The six steps

  1. 1

    Register online and upload documents

    Buy a PIN online at medadmgujarat.org, then fill in your details on the same site.

    Enter NEET-PG details + PINFill details, upload documentsCreate passwordConfirm detailsPrint registration form
  2. 2

    Get documents verified at a help centre

    Visit any of the 17 help centres with your originals. Your registration is confirmed only after this.

  3. 3

    Check the merit list

    ACPPGMEC publishes the merit list of eligible candidates. Check that your merit number and category match your registration.

  4. 4

    Fill choices and see your allotment

    Choice filling opens online for each round. After it closes, log in with your user ID to see your allotment.

  5. 5

    Pay one term of fees

    Pay six months of tuition fees by cash or DD at a designated Axis Bank branch, or online, within the time limit.

  6. 6

    Submit originals to confirm admission

    Take your allotment letter, fee receipt and original documents to a help centre. Your admission is confirmed here.

Who can apply

You must have qualified NEET-PG 2026. Beyond that, eligibility depends on where you studied MBBS.

MBBS from Gujarat

  • Degree from a university set up under Gujarat law and located in Gujarat.

MBBS from outside Gujarat

  • From another state or abroad, meeting NMC eligibility.
  • 12th with B or AB group, from the Gujarat Board or a school in Gujarat.
  • Born in Gujarat, or hold a Gujarat domicile certificate.

Based on earlier versions of the eligibility notice. Check the 2026 eligibility PDF on the official site for any changes.

Watch out for these

Certificates must be from Gujarat

Caste, non-creamy layer and EWS certificates are accepted only if issued by Gujarat State authorities.

Check the certificate date

This notice says SEBC non-creamy layer and EWS certificates must be dated on or after 01/04/2024. Another 2026 notice reportedly says on or after 01/04/2026. Get a fresh certificate if you can.

Internship cut-off date

This notice says internship must be complete on or before 30/09/2026. Some reports of the 2026 notice show 31/05/2026. Confirm which applies.

Carry all marksheets

The upload list asks for final-year marksheets, but the final submission list asks for all years. Carry every year, every attempt.

Disability certificate

An original is asked for at verification and a photocopy at final submission. Carry both.

NRI certificate

It appears only in the final submission list. Prepare it from the start anyway.

Not covered in this notice

Seat resignation, fee forfeiture, upgrades between rounds, stray rounds and service bonds. Read the PG admission rules and their amendments on the official site.

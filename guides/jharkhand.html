Who can apply

You need to fit one of these three routes.

MBBS from a Jharkhand college

Any medical college or institute in Jharkhand.

Jharkhand resident, MBBS in Bihar

Admitted to a Bihar medical college around or before the formation of Jharkhand, as set out in Sankalp No. 9 dated 21.11.2022. Local Resident certificate required.

Jharkhand resident serving as a state government doctor

MBBS from any college in India. Local Resident certificate required.

Everyone must also have

  • Registration with NMC or the Jharkhand State Medical Council. Temporary registration is accepted, but permanent registration is needed by the last admission date or the seat is cancelled.
  • Rotating internship completed by 30 September 2026.

Ways candidates lose a seat or their category

  • Choosing a different category from the NEET-PG form. You will be treated as Unreserved, unless your caste differs between the Central and State lists and you upload the original certificate.
  • A caste certificate from any other officer, or any other supporting document. These are not accepted.
  • Wrong information at registration, or missing originals at joining. The admission is cancelled and the security deposit is kept.
  • Not getting permanent registration by the last admission date.

When the deposit comes back

  • Refunded if you take a valid admission, or if no seat is allotted to you in any round.
  • Forfeited if you are allotted a seat in Round 2 or a later round and don't join.
  • Forfeited if admission is cancelled for wrong information or missing original certificates.

The notice does not say outright what happens if you leave a Round 1 seat. It implies the deposit is safe, but confirm before relying on it. MCC (New Delhi) guidelines for NEET-PG 2026 apply to all rounds.

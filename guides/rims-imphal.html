RIMS Imphal — MD/MS Admissions Guide

Regional Institute of Medical Sciences, Imphal, Manipur. It is a central government institute under the Union Health Ministry. Fees are very low, but leaving the course costs a lot.

Money & bond

Fees at admission₹39,500
Fees each following year₹20,800
Leaving the seat in year 1₹10 lakh
Leaving after completing year 1₹15 lakh
Leaving in year 3₹20 lakh
Bond for the 1-year senior residency (non-sponsored)₹20 lakh
If you leave, you must also pay back any salary already received. Your original MBBS certificates are kept by RIMS until you finish the course, and are only returned after any penalty is paid. Transferring to another college is not allowed.

Daily life rules

  • Biometric attendance twice a day, between 8–9 AM and 4–6 PM. Missing either punch counts as absent.
  • Leave: 20 days paid plus 5 academic days per year. You need 80% attendance to sit the exam.
  • Unauthorised absence costs ₹500 a day.
  • Hostels: about 325 PG beds for men and 304 for women. Outstation students get priority.
  • Hostel curfews are between 6:30 and 8 PM depending on gender and season. Late hospital duty is exempt.

Points to check before choosing

  • Thin departments: Psychiatry has 2 faculty for 3 seats. Dermatology and Transfusion Medicine are each headed by an Assistant Professor "in-charge".
  • Reporting rule: the prospectus says students need the Director's permission before taking institute matters to the police, press or public. This does not override national anti-ragging helplines.
  • Dates: all admission and joining dates say "to be notified". Last year RIMS issued its PG circular in late November and ran rounds until January.
Bottom line: RIMS suits a candidate who is sure of the branch and willing to stay in Imphal for about 4 years (3 years of PG plus the bonded year). It is risky for anyone who might drop the seat for a better option in a later round.

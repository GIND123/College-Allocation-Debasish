General instructions Clauses 1, 4

Seats covered are those in Tamil Nadu Government Medical Colleges affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and in DNB-accredited institutions of Government headquarters hospitals and sub-district hospitals. Everything about the process is published at www.tnmedicalselection.net; the Selection Committee does not contact candidates individually, and is not responsible for consequences of not following notices on that site.

Documents are mandatory with the application Following W.P. No.27151 of 2022 and W.M.P. Nos.26366, 26368 and 26370 of 2022 of the High Court of Madras, all relevant documents listed in Annexure I must be uploaded along with the online application. No offline submission of documents is accepted, and incomplete applications or applications with documents that are not clearly visible are summarily rejected.

Submitting the online application means the candidate has read, agreed to and accepted the scheme of counselling and its terms and conditions. Once final submission is complete, particulars cannot be changed under any circumstances, and no request for reconsideration of a rejected application is entertained.

Only in-service candidates may apply for the State quota seats, per NBEMS letter Reference No. NBEMS/EDO/2022/2705 dated 01.06.2022. Medical officers working in any government institution must get the service proforma certified by the designated authority with date and seal and upload it; otherwise the application is summarily rejected (W.P. No.27151/2022 dated 14.11.2022).

Candidates seeking the Persons with Disabilities special category must upload the filled and certified special category form, certified within three months. If the PwD medical certificate is not uploaded, the application is considered under the general category only.

The entire study period is treated as study leave, following approval and permission from the head of the department as per State rules.

Admissions and resignations are made through online mode only. Anything done offline is not accepted, and hospitals taking offline admissions or resignations do so at their own legal risk. Resignation applies to candidates allotted in Rounds 1 and 2.

Candidates taking All India quota DNB seats through MCC / NBE, including in-service candidates of other States, also execute a two-year bond on completing the course. Candidates should also read the NBEMS information bulletin at natboard.edu.in for qualifying criteria.

Eligibility Clauses 5–7

Candidates must qualify in NEET PG 2026 conducted by the National Board of Examinations. Eligibility follows the criteria prescribed by the Director General of Health Services and the Post Graduate Medical Education Regulations as amended.

Candidates must be citizens of India and must have undergone the MBBS course, including the whole CRMI / CRRI internship, in the State of Tamil Nadu. Those claiming Tamil Nadu nativity who completed MBBS in another State must upload a nativity certificate issued by the competent authority of the Revenue and Disaster Management Department, Government of Tamil Nadu, with official seal. Dual nativity leads to cancellation of allotment and legal action at any stage.

Candidates claiming community reservation must upload both their own community certificate and the community certificate of one parent, each issued by the competent authority of the Revenue and Disaster Management Department with official seal.

Qualification and registration

Candidates must hold an MBBS degree from the Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University or another university recognised by the Medical Council of India. Those qualified from other universities must produce an eligibility certificate from the Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University, Guindy, Chennai 600 032 at the time of counselling or admission.

Candidates who cleared MBBS and completed CRMI / CRRI on or before 30.09.2026 are eligible, and must hold a permanent medical registration certificate of a State council or of India, or a provisional registration certificate, at the time of counselling or admission. Candidates currently pursuing a diploma, including under the All India quota, may apply for this same academic year if the diploma is completed before the counselling process begins on 30.09.2026.

Course duration is three years for DNB Broad Speciality (Post MBBS) and two years for the Post MBBS Diploma, which was introduced in 2020–2021.

Additional documents required of every candidate

  • Copy of Aadhaar card
  • Copy of driving licence, voter's ID or passport as a supporting document

An application without these supporting documents is summarily rejected.

Non-eligibility Clauses 8–11

A candidate who joins a 2026–2027 DNB Broad Speciality or Post MBBS Diploma seat in any branch and discontinues in any round on or after Round 2 is not eligible to apply in a subsequent academic year, and is treated as having discontinued, with the discontinuation fee under clause 23(b) payable.

A candidate allotted a seat in Round 3 must join. Not joining carries the same consequence: ineligibility for subsequent academic years under NBE norms, and the discontinuation fee under clause 23(c).

A candidate who already holds a diploma cannot apply for the Post MBBS Diploma seats, but can apply for DNB Broad Speciality (Post MBBS). A candidate who already holds a postgraduate degree, DNB or equivalent cannot apply for either. Candidates belonging to States other than Tamil Nadu are not eligible.

Suppressing or hiding any eligibility or non-eligibility fact results in cancellation of the allotment order and appropriate legal or disciplinary action.

Service candidates Clause 12

Candidates fill the online proforma, download it, and have it verified and signed with official seal by the competent forwarding authority. The authority must issue a complete service proforma including leave and deputation in detail, and the candidate must upload it within the stipulated time.

A service candidate applying under the service quota must have at least five years of service remaining on completing the course.

Who counts as a service candidate

  • Medical officers selected by the TNPSC or the Medical Services Recruitment Board through competitive written examination or special qualifying examination, appointed in the Tamil Nadu Medical Services, with at least two years of continuous service as on 31.03.2026.
  • Medical officers serving in local bodies in Tamil Nadu with at least two years of continuous service as on 31.03.2026.

Who does not

  • Temporarily appointed candidates without two years of continuous service as on 31.03.2026 — such applications are summarily rejected even if certified inadvertently by the competent authority.
  • Candidates working in institutions under the Registered Societies Act and in self-financing institutions, who are treated as non-service candidates.
  • Private candidates, who are not eligible for the in-service doctors quota at all.
Suppression of employment A medical officer in a government institution or local body who applies as a private candidate while concealing that employment has the application summarily rejected, and any allotment cancelled.

Rank list Clauses 16–17

The State rank list is drawn on the marks obtained in NEET PG 2026. The Selection Committee publishes provisional rank lists on the official website after scrutiny of all applications received for 2026–2027.

Counselling rounds Clauses 18–28

There are four rounds: Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 and a stray vacancy round. All allotment is online, by rank, applying the rule of reservation. Re-allotment is permitted in Rounds 2 and 3 only. Mutual transfer and individual requests for transfer of college are not permitted under any circumstances.

A candidate who did not register or choice-lock in an earlier round may still attend the later rounds. A candidate allotted a seat who did not join, or who resigned within the stipulated time, remains eligible for the next round.

Allotted candidates download the provisional allotment order and join at the allotted DNB institution — a district headquarters hospital or sub-district hospital — on or before the stipulated date and time, carrying the originals listed under Annexure I. Failure to report physically within the stipulated date is treated as not joined and the seat is declared vacant.

At reporting, candidates sign a self-declaration that selection or admission is liable to forfeiture if suppression of facts or misrepresentation is found at any time. If suppression is found later, the admission is liable to cancellation and the candidate cannot apply for these courses for the next two academic years.

Round 2 in detail

Round 2 fills vacancies arising from seats not joined or not allotted, and any newly sanctioned seats, by rank and communal reservation. Candidates who retained a Round 1 seat beyond the stipulated date, who took a re-allotment in Round 2, or who were newly allotted in Round 2 may vacate within the date given by the Selection Committee. Those who joined a Round 2 seat may resign within that date.

Virtual vacancies A seat held from Round 1 by a candidate who opted for upgradation in Round 2 appears as a virtual vacancy in Round 2, and anyone may fill it during choice filling. It is allotted only if the Round 1 candidate actually vacates, at which point the virtual vacancy becomes a real one, open to all in order of merit. The same rule applies to upgradation within Round 2.

Round 3

Remaining vacancies after Round 2 are filled from the rank list. A candidate allotted and joined in Round 1 or 2 from locked choices may go for upgradation in Round 3. A candidate allotted in Round 3 is ineligible for any further counselling, so options should be filled with care and unwanted options left out.

If the candidate is allotted but does not join — whether or not the allotment order was downloaded — the registration fee, security deposit and tuition fee are forfeited. If DGHS / MCC releases the joined list of All India quota candidates in Round 3, those candidates are blocked and the State Round 3 result is released.

Who may take part in Round 3

  • Candidates not allotted in Rounds 1 and 2.
  • Candidates allotted, joined or not joined in Rounds 1 and 2 of the State quota and the All India quota.
  • Candidates who did not register or choice-lock in Round 1 or 2.
  • Candidates who joined in Round 1 or 2 of State counselling but resigned in Round 2 within the stipulated date.

Who may not

  • Candidates who resigned after Round 2 beyond the stipulated time.

Registration fee for Round 3 — ₹1,000

  • Payable by candidates who were allotted and did not join, and by candidates registering freshly for Round 3.
  • Not payable by candidates who were not allotted in Rounds 1 and 2.

Stray vacancy round

Conducted by the Selection Committee, with a ₹1,000 registration fee on the same basis: payable by those allotted and not joined or resigned in Rounds 1 and 2 and by fresh registrants, not payable by candidates not allotted in Rounds 1, 2 and 3. Candidates who discontinued with penalty after the stipulated time cannot take part in any further round.

Eligible are candidates not allotted in Rounds 1, 2 and 3; candidates allotted but not joined in Rounds 1 and 2 of the State and All India quotas; and candidates who did not register or choice-lock in the earlier rounds.

Not eligible for the stray round

  • Candidates holding a PG seat from Round 1, 2 or 3 of State counselling.
  • Candidates holding a seat allotted in Rounds 1, 2 or 3 of the All India quota, a deemed university, or DNB counselling conducted by NBE.
  • Candidates allotted in the stray round of the All India quota, a deemed university or NBE DNB counselling.
  • Candidates allotted in Round 3 of State counselling who did not join.
Stray round allotment A seat allotted in the stray round according to the candidate's own choice filling must be joined without fail. Further rounds may be conducted if directed by DGHS.

Seats and reservation Clauses 29–30

Of the total recognised or permitted seats in Government Medical Colleges and Government hospitals, 50% go to the All India quota for allotment by the Director General of Health Services, New Delhi, and the remaining 50% are allotted under the State quota during State counselling.

Selection follows the merit list and the rule of reservation of the Government of Tamil Nadu. Where a speciality has more than one and fewer than eight seats, the 200-point roster method applies, under G.O. (Ms.) No.241, Personnel and Administrative Reforms (K) Department, dated 29.10.2007 — subject to the final verdict of the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal 6049–6050/2010. Where only one seat is available in a speciality, it is allotted on rank alone.

How unfilled reserved seats move

  • SCA seats may be filled by SC candidates on merit and the reverse, per G.O. (Ms.) No.55, P&AR Department, dated 08.04.2010, so SC candidates may choice-lock unfilled SCA seats.
  • ST seats left unfilled for want of eligible ST candidates may be filled by SC candidates on merit, per G.O. (Ms.) No.77, Health and Family Welfare Department, dated 24.02.2011.
  • BCM seats left unfilled after the merit list is exhausted go to BC candidates on merit, without exceeding the overall BC quota, per G.O. (Ms.) No.30, Health and Family Welfare Department, dated 18.01.2012.
  • Where more qualified Arunthathiyars remain after the seats reserved for them are filled, they compete with other Scheduled Castes on inter-se merit, per G.O. (Ms.) No.65, P&AR (K) Department, dated 27.05.2009.

Persons with Disabilities allotment is done ahead of the general category, with no re-allotment or change of college. If enough eligible special category candidates are not available, those seats revert to the general category; where a seat is available in a speciality under the special category the candidate cannot choose to remain on a waiting list, and any not-joined or discontinued special category seat is added to the general category.

Service candidates, State quota

Service candidates are paid a stipend during the study period and are obliged to serve the Government of Tamil Nadu until superannuation after completing the course. All Tamil Nadu service candidates must have more than five years of service left after completing the course.

A candidate who discontinues service by resignation or voluntary retirement after completing the course, or who does not join service immediately after it, pays compensation of ₹40,00,000, or a sum proportionate to the period left unserved. Interest is payable on the compensation at the Government of Tamil Nadu housing loan rate, calculated from the date the demand is issued. Absconding from service makes the individual liable to prosecution for compensation under the Revenue Recovery Act. The bond becomes infructuous once the candidate serves until superannuation.

All India 50% quota, through MCC and NBE

These candidates also receive a stipend and are obliged to serve the Government of Tamil Nadu for two years, within three years from the date of completing the PG course, if the Government requires their services. During that period they are paid on par with fresh recruits of the Tamil Nadu Medical Services, and the bond becomes infructuous once the two years are served.

A candidate posted to the Tamil Nadu Medical Service who does not comply pays compensation of ₹40,00,000 for the Broad Speciality course or ₹20,00,000 for the Post MBBS Diploma, or a proportionate sum for the period left unserved, with the same interest rate and the same liability under the Revenue Recovery Act. A candidate who discontinues the course pays the full discontinuation fee under clause 23(b) and returns the stipend received.

Where the Government requires the services of such a candidate who is unable to serve for any reason, the original certificates are retained by the Government and released only after the expiry of that period.

Why the bond exists The prospectus records the reasoning: the Government spends a large amount on postgraduate medical education, levies nominal fees and pays a stipend, and expects these seats not to be wasted and trained specialists to serve the sick, the poor and the needy. The undertaking is taken at admission to make sure those services are available.

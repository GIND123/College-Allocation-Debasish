Who can apply

You need a NEET-PG 2026 qualifying result, a recognised MBBS and to meet the Government of Punjab notifications for the quota you claim. Qualifying or a good rank alone gives no right to a seat: the principal of the allotted college decides final eligibility after checking your originals.

  • Category codes on the form: Open/General 11, SC 12, BC 13, PwBD 14, EWS 15. You may claim more than one. A category not claimed on the form, or claimed without valid papers, is treated as Open. It cannot be changed after the correction window.
  • SC, BC and EWS certificates must be issued by the Government of Punjab; EWS certificates from other states are not accepted.
  • PwBD needs a certificate from a centre designated under the RPwD Act (list on the MCC site), or from the State Medical Assessment Board at GGS Medical College, Faridkot.
  • PCMS in-service candidates (Punjab Civil Medical Services) apply with an NOC and an eligibility certificate from the competent authority, and add incentive marks to their NEET-PG score.
  • Sikh and Christian minority quota: your minority status is verified by SGRD Institute, Amritsar (Sikh) or CMC Ludhiana (Christian) before counselling; only verified candidates are considered for those seats.
  • NRI quota: get a provisional eligibility certificate from BFUHS first (US$1,500), then apply (another US$1,500), and submit the hard copy at the university.

How seats are shared

Half the seats in government colleges go to the All India Quota through MCC; the state half is filled by BFUHS. Private colleges offer government quota, management quota and NRI quota seats, and the minority colleges (SGRD Amritsar, CMC Ludhiana) their own minority seats.

  • Reserved-category candidates with marks at or above the general qualifying score are considered first for open seats, then for their category.
  • In private colleges, an eligible candidate is considered first against the management quota, then the institutional preference (IP) quota or any other quota. A candidate eligible for both an IP seat and a minority seat is considered for the IP seat first.
  • The complete seat matrix (by speciality, college, quota and category) is shown on the BFUHS site before choice filling. The prospectus's distribution of seats is only tentative.

Step by step

  1. Apply online at bfuhs.ac.in and pay the application fee. Upload a recent photo and signature (30 KB each). Only one application per candidate; offline forms are not accepted.
  2. PCMS, NRI and minority candidates also submit papers at the Admission Branch, BFUHS, Faridkot, or get verified by SGRD Amritsar or CMC Ludhiana. In Round 2 these closed on 17 November.
  3. Check the merit list and raise any objection in person at BFUHS by the deadline.
  4. Fill your choices of course, speciality and college in order of preference. Seats are allotted on merit, reservation, eligibility and your choices.
  5. Allotment: download the provisional allotment letter from your login.
  6. Pay the six-month fee online and report to the college with originals and the fee receipt. Not paying or not reporting in time cancels the allotment and the seat goes to the next round.

How the rounds work

Third round is final

If you join a seat in Round 3, you cannot resign afterwards and cannot take part in any further counselling.

Round 1

  • Free exit (only in this round)
  • Surrender by written request to the principal, within the set period

Round 2

  • Give willingness and pay the security deposit
  • Fill fresh choices to upgrade
  • Happy with your seat? You need not take part

Round 3 and later

  • Fresh willingness and choices
  • Joining is final: no resignation
  • Fill choices in your genuine order, not just from the vacancy list: more seats open up during allotment through upgrades, resignations and conversions.
  • If you are upgraded, the fee you already paid is adjusted; you pay only the balance. A deposit already paid is not asked for again.
  • Registered candidates who sat out Round 1, or got no seat, can still take part in Round 2.

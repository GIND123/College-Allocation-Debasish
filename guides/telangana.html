Who can apply

  • Telangana local candidate — studied in Telangana for at least 4 consecutive years ending with the qualifying exam.
  • Indian national, PIO, or OCI card holder.
  • MBBS from an NMC-recognised college in Telangana, or from Siddhartha Medical College, Vijayawada.
  • Permanent registration with the Telangana State Medical Council.
  • Internship completed on or before 30 Sep 2026.
  • Qualified NEET-PG 2026 at or above the cutoff (see below).

Not eligible

  • Candidates from other states.
  • Those who took MBBS admission in Telangana under the non-local quota.
  • Anyone currently studying a PG Degree, Diploma, or DNB course.
Special case: a Telangana local for MBBS admission who studied at a state-wide institution outside Telangana is still treated as local.

Seats are in KNRUHS-affiliated medical colleges and NIMS, Hyderabad.

Bonds you will sign at joining

  • ₹50 lakh if you leave the course midway, plus repayment of stipend received (all candidates).
  • 1-year government service after the course: ₹20 lakh bond for Degree, ₹10 lakh for Diploma (not for in-service candidates).
  • Quitting after the free-exit date: ₹50 lakh penalty and barred for 3 years from KNRUHS PG counselling.

Original certificates stay with the college until you finish the course. A notarised anti-drug undertaking (Annexure IV) is also required.

How to apply

Use a computer, not a phone or tablet. Keep your mobile with you for OTPs.
  1. Register mobile & email on tspgmed.tsche.in using your NEET roll no., rank and date of birth. Verify both with OTP. Done only once.
  2. Candidate registration & payment. Enter caste category and pay online. Don't refresh or close the page until you see the success message. Note the registration number sent to your phone and email.
  3. Fill the application using your roll no. and registration no. Upload photo and signature. Use "Save and Exit" — you can edit until verification starts.
  4. Upload certificates as PDFs under 500 KB each. Verification happens only once for all rounds, so upload everything now.
  5. Print the application and keep it with your originals plus one set of self-attested copies for joining.
Applications missing a mandatory certificate, the photo, or the signature at the declaration are rejected automatically. False information can lead to cancellation and criminal action.

Watch out

  • Use the official prospectus. Some copies circulating online carry a paid-counselling WhatsApp number on every page — that is not from KNRUHS. Download from knruhs.telangana.gov.in.
  • Medical registration must be permanent, even though the online form shows a "Permanent/Temporary" option.
  • The prospectus has a typo in the Diploma attendance rule: 80% means 501 of 626 working days, not 730.
  • No transfers between colleges or courses, and no switching Degree ↔ Diploma, once admitted.
  • No private practice or part-time jobs during the course. Minimum 80% attendance, 20 days' leave per year.
  • Students may not approach courts, NMC, or government about grievances without the institution's permission, and may not criticise it in the media — read the Discipline clause before joining.

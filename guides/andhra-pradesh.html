Local and non-local status

Candidates from other states can't apply unless they meet AP's local or non-local rules.

The two local areas

  • Andhra University (AU) area: erstwhile Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur and Prakasam districts.
  • Sri Venkateswara University (SVU) area: erstwhile Anantapur, Kurnool, Chittoor, Cuddapah and Nellore districts.

Who counts as local

  • Studied in that area for 4 consecutive years ending with the year of first appearing for Final MBBS Part-II, or lived there for 4 years before that exam.
  • If neither applies, 7 years of study or residence in AP decides the area (where you spent the most time, or the most recent if tied).
  • Your own residence counts, not your parents'.

Seat split

  • 85% of seats go to local candidates of each area.
  • The remaining 15% are "unreserved". Per the government memo of 20-07-2024, these are now for AP locals only.
  • Non-locals can't take local seats. Non-locals with AP domicile (APNL) can compete for unreserved seats.
  • Siddhartha Medical College, Vijayawada is a state-wide institution: its 85% is split AU 65.62% and SVU 34.38%, and 15% is unreserved.

Who can claim the 15% unreserved seats

  • 10 years' residence in AP (yours or a parent's), excluding time studying or working outside the state.
  • Children or spouses of people currently employed by AP or Central Government, PSUs, local bodies or universities within AP.
  • Candidates themselves employed in such bodies within AP.
Conflicting wording The notification says the 15% unreserved seats are both "exclusively for local candidates" (point 6) and "for local/non-local" candidates (point 7). If you're from outside AP, confirm with the regulations helpline before paying the non-refundable fee.

Seats and reservations

How seats are divided

  • Government colleges: 50% All India Quota, 50% state Competent Authority quota.
  • Private unaided non-minority colleges: 50% Competent Authority, 50% Management Quota.
  • New government colleges (from 2023-24): of the state's 50%, half is Competent Authority (general), 35% self-financing and 15% NRI.
  • The seat matrix is published on the website before web options open.

Bonds and penalties

  • Non-service candidates: one year of rural or government service in AP within 18 months of getting the PG degree. Breaking it costs ₹40 lakh.
  • In-service candidates: 10 years of government service after the course. Breaking it costs ₹50 lakh plus all pay received during study, with interest. Certificates are held by the department until the 10 years are done.
  • Discontinuing is only allowed after counselling closes.
  • Migration or transfer between colleges or courses, or between degree and diploma, is not allowed.

In-service quota

  • Seat share: 15% of clinical and 30% of non-clinical seats in the state quota.
  • Minimum regular service as on 01-07-2026: 2 years in tribal areas, 3 years in rural areas, or 6 years overall in AP government health facilities. Unauthorised absence and leave without allowance don't count.
  • Age: must not have turned 50 by 01-07-2026 (born on or before 01-07-1976 is not eligible).
  • Paperwork: Annexure IV-A (self-declaration) and IV-B (signed by the appointing authority), plus the bond with two income-tax-paying sureties, one of them a government employee.
  • Deputation with pay: up to 36 months in your whole career.
  • Unfilled in-service seats go to non-service candidates of the same category after Mop-up.

Counselling rounds

Four rounds: Phase I, Phase II, Phase III (Mop-up) and a Stray round. All web options are online; there's no limit on how many you choose.

Phases I and II

  • Pay ₹25,600 to download the allotment letter, then report with original certificates by the stated date.
  • If you don't join an allotted seat, that seat is removed from your options in later rounds.
  • If you join, it becomes your default last option, and you can upgrade until Phase III. Free exit is allowed on the notified schedule.

Phase III (Mop-up)

  • A refundable ₹50,000 security deposit is needed to take part.
  • Getting a seat here ends your counselling for the year, including Management Quota, whether you join or not.
  • Not joining a Mop-up seat means you lose the deposit.
  • Anyone who joined an All India Quota seat up to Round 3 is blocked from state Mop-up and Stray.

Stray round (non-service only)

  • Fresh choice filling; earlier choices don't carry over.
  • All seats are treated as unreserved open general.
  • Not joining an allotted stray seat means you lose the deposit and are barred from AP PG counselling (CQ and MQ) for one year.
Deposit refund The ₹50,000 is refunded after admissions close if you join your seat or aren't allotted one. It's kept if you don't join or your admission is cancelled for wrong information.

How to apply on the portal

Use a desktop or laptop, not a phone.

  1. Register: click Registration, enter your NEET roll number and date of birth, and click Get Details. Check your mobile and email, set a password, and confirm with the OTP. Note down your Registration ID.
  2. Log in with your Registration ID and password.
  3. Go to Services → Application Submission. Fill all four tabs: Personal Details, Payment, Local/Non-local, Upload Documents. Skipping any tab makes you ineligible.
  4. Pay the fee. If money is deducted but payment fails, click Verify and Continue and wait 15 minutes before retrying. Failed amounts come back within 7 working days.
  5. Region is filled in automatically from your MBBS college.
  6. Upload documents, click Save Documents, then Preview Application.
  7. Check everything, then Final Submit. Nothing can be edited after this.
  8. Use Services to check payment status, print the provisional application, and track application status.
Region traps
  • MBBS in Telangana: you must claim "Migrated from TG to AP" or APNL. Choosing "No" makes you ineligible.
  • MBBS outside AP: you must claim "APNL with AP Domicile" and upload proof. Choosing "No" makes you ineligible.

Keep your registered mobile number active until admissions close. Never share your login, password or OTP; anything done through your login counts as done by you.

  • Tuition and other fees: ₹1,88,000 per year (₹18,800 of it non-refundable if you slide to another college). Years 2 and 3 are deducted from stipend.

SVIMS bonds

  • Leaving the course: ₹5 lakh plus all stipend received.
  • Skipping the one-year compulsory service: ₹40 lakh.
  • Two gazetted government officers must sign as sureties.

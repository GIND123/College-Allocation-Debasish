Getting out of a seat, round by round

The rules are the same in both quotas. Freedom shrinks each round, so candidates should only list colleges they would actually join from Round 3 on.

RoundCan you leave?Cost of leaving
Round 1Yes Free exit, or resign within the windowNothing forfeited. Still eligible for later rounds
Round 2Within window Resign before the stipulated dateNot joining: lose ₹1,000 registration. Resigning after the date: ₹15L / ₹10L penalty + all fees, and out of Round 3
Round 3No Must joinLose registration, security deposit and tuition. After joining, full penalty. No further rounds
StrayNo Must joinSame as Round 3

Cross-quota rule (GQ prospectus): a candidate allotted a GQ seat in Round 3 cannot claim an MQ seat in MQ Round 3.

Documents to upload with the form

A Madras High Court order makes upload at the application stage mandatory. Missing or unclear copies mean rejection with no appeal.

Everyone

  • NEET-PG 2026 score card and admit card
  • MBBS degree or provisional certificate; CRRI completion
  • NMC or State Medical Council registration
  • Aadhaar, plus passport, voter ID or driving licence
  • The same photo used on the NEET-PG form
  • TN Dr. MGR University eligibility certificate, if MBBS was from another university

GQ extra

  • TN nativity certificate
  • Two community certificates: own and one parent's, from TN Revenue
  • Parents' Aadhaar and school certificates
  • Service candidates: certified service proforma, TNPSC / MRB posting order
  • PwD: medical board certificate under 3 months old

MQ extra

  • Postcard photo 4×6 inch, 50–300 KB
  • Minority certificate from a Tahsildar, with nativity certificate (if claiming minority)
  • OCI card, if applicable

NRI extra

  • Sponsor's NRI status from the Indian Embassy
  • Relationship certificate from Revenue, plus sponsor's passport
  • Sponsor's NRE bank passbook
  • Ward declaration (Annexure III)
  • Court guardianship order, if the sponsor is a guardian

NRI quota in brief

  • The sponsor must be a parent, spouse, blood relative (uncles, aunts, grandparents, first cousins) or a court-appointed guardian, and must be an NRI.
  • The Embassy verifies documents. Fake papers lead to seat cancellation, criminal action, a 3-year bar and no refund.
  • Unfilled NRI seats go back to the management pool.

GQ In-service doctors

Who counts as a service candidate

  • Medical officers selected by TNPSC or MRB through a competitive exam, working in Tamil Nadu Medical Services
  • Medical officers in Tamil Nadu local bodies
  • Both need 2 years of continuous service as on 31.03.2026, and at least 5 years of service left when the course ends
  • Temporary appointees, and staff of registered societies or self-financing colleges, apply as non-service candidates
  • A government doctor who applies as a private candidate and hides it loses the application or seat

What they get

  • Half of the state-quota seats are reserved for them. The other half is open to everyone
  • Extra marks: 10% of NEET-PG marks per year of service in hilly, remote, difficult or rural areas, up to 30%
  • Half of PG Diploma seats go to officers with 3+ years in such areas. They must serve 2 more years there afterwards
  • Full salary during the course
  • Unfilled service seats go to general candidates

GQ How reserved seats are filled

Seats in a branchMethod
1 seatGoes by rank only
2 to 7 seats200-point roster (the order in which each community gets its turn)
8 or morePercentages: OC 31, BC 30 (BC Muslim 3.5 within it), MBC/DNC 20, SC 18 (16% of this for Arunthathiyar), ST 1
  • Unfilled Arunthathiyar seats go to SC, and the reverse. Extra Arunthathiyar candidates also compete for general SC seats on merit.
  • Unfilled ST seats go to SC. Unfilled BC Muslim seats go to BC.
  • So SC candidates should also choose SCA and ST seats, and BC candidates should also choose BCM seats.
  • PwD seats (5% in government colleges) are allotted before general ones. No change of college afterwards. Unfilled PwD seats go to general.
  • No mutual transfers or college-change requests.

Course length and who cannot apply GQ MQ

Course length

  • MD / MS: 3 years
  • Holders of a 2-year diploma: 2 years in the same specialty, 3 in another
  • No reduction for 1-year diplomas, Clinical Pathology or Diabetology diplomas
  • PG Diploma: 2 years

Not eligible

  • Anyone currently doing a PG degree, DNB or diploma
  • Anyone who has finished a PG degree or DNB
  • A diploma holder applying for another diploma (a degree is allowed)
  • Diploma students may apply for a degree only if they finish before 9 Oct 2026
  • Absence of 15 days or more without leave counts as leaving the course, with the penalty if past the cut-off date.
  • Ragging, strikes or agitation lead to expulsion and criminal action.

Problems found in the documents

  • Redistributed copies. Every page of both files carries a WhatsApp stamp advertising paid counselling. Both prospectuses warn against agents. Use the clean copies from the official site.
  • MQ reservation is unclear. Clause 20 says seats follow the rule of reservation; clause 27 says vacancies are filled by rank. Worth confirming with the Selection Committee.
  • MQ Round 2 table wording. The penalty row says "opting for upgradation"; the GQ version says "not opting". One of them is a typo.
  • MQ Round 3 non-joining. Clause 11(b) treats it as discontinuation with the full penalty; the round table only lists forfeiture of fees.
  • Outdated regulator. MQ clauses 32–34 still refer to the MCI; GQ uses the NMC.
  • Missing annexures. Both cite an Annexure IV on ragging that isn't included. GQ mentions a special-category (PwD) form that isn't attached. MQ clause 16(m) is an orphaned line.
  • GQ PwD limit. Clause 35 bars disability above 80%; Annexure II allows case-by-case review for locomotor disability.
  • Cut-off scores. Check 262 / 244 / 226 against the official NBE 2026 notice before relying on them.

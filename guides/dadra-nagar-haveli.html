👥 Who can get which seat

  • AIQ seats (23): filled by MCC, open to everyone through MCC counselling.
  • UT seats (17): only for domicile candidates of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
  • Outside candidates: only Management or NRI seats, or leftover seats in the stray vacancy round.
  • Unfilled AIQ seats returned to the college go to DNH/DD domicile candidates on rotation, not to outsiders.

Priority order for UT seats

  • 1st (from Round 1): UT domicile who did MBBS at NAMO, or via central-pool seats given to the UT before 2019.
  • 2nd (from Round 2): other UT domicile MBBS graduates first, then UT domicile foreign medical graduates.
  • 3rd (stray round only): any MBBS graduate from an NMC-recognised college.

  • Leaving a Radiology AIQ seat could cost about ₹65 lakh (₹60 L tuition + ₹5 L penalty).
  • The penalty also covers "failing to complete the course", not just leaving.
  • Unpaid dues can be recovered like unpaid land tax.
  • If you don't want an offered seat, you must tell the Dean in writing within one day.

  • Penalty waiver possible only on genuine medical grounds, via a Special Medical Board.

🔍 Gaps & contradictions in the documents

Quota percentages: the text says Management 15% and NRI 10%, but the seat table gives about 9% (4/46) and 4% (2/46).
Security deposit: the prospectus mentions a ₹2 lakh application deposit and also a ₹1 lakh refundable deposit in the fee table, without explaining the difference.
Penalty wording: the AIQ notice says tuition for the entire course; the prospectus says remaining tuition. Get this clarified in writing.
Wrong cross-reference: Clause 3.2 points to "Clause 10.3, Other Conditions", but that section is Clause 11.3.
Fees without seats: Management fees are listed for General Medicine and Paediatrics, which have no management seats (likely for unfilled NRI seats converted to management).

✅ Bottom line

UT domicile candidates: a strong option with protected seats, but plan for the service bond and the high fees.
AIQ candidates: costs close to a private or deemed university (₹21–60 L tuition), though with no service bond. Don't fill it in MCC choices expecting government-level fees.
Outside candidates (Mgmt/NRI): ₹1.8–2.1 crore for Management seats plus strict exit penalties. Think carefully before applying.

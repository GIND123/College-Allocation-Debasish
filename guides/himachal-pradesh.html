Paying is not applying

Your application counts only after you press Final Submit and download the application PDF. Money leaving your account is not enough. After final submission nothing can be edited; a correction means a new application with a new email ID and a second fee.

How seats are shared

The counselling fills 50% of PG seats in IGMC Shimla, RPGMC Tanda, SLBSGMC Ner Chowk, YSPGMC Nahan, PJNGMC Chamba and RKGMC Hamirpur, plus all seats at MMMC Kumarhatti, Solan.

Bonus marks for in-service doctors

In-service doctors get bonus marks on top of their NEET-PG score for each year of field posting (calculated day by day), up to a maximum of 30%. Bonus marks count only after the minimum service for that area is complete. Urban postings earn none.

The service bond (Govt. colleges)

Under HP PG/SS Policy 2025, every State Quota PG in a government college signs a bond to serve Himachal Pradesh after the course.

WhoServiceBond amount
Direct candidate2 years (incl. 1 yr field)₹40 lakh
Sponsored GDO, PG in HP4 years (incl. 1 yr field)₹40 lakh
Sponsored GDO, INI sponsored seat5 years (incl. 1 yr field)₹40 lakh
  • You also hand over an undated cheque for the bond amount, and your original MBBS degree is kept until the bond is served.
  • Leaving the course midway: ₹10 lakh + 18% yearly interest + all stipend or salary drawn. Sponsored GDOs are also barred from PG entrance exams for 5 years.
  • Taking a Senior Residency or Tutor post in an HP government college during the bond extends it to 3 years of SR.
  • SR at AIMSS Chamiana, or SR in Radiodiagnosis at any HP government college except IGMC, counts as the field posting.
  • Breaking the bond can also lead to cancellation of medical registration.

How the rounds work

Most common mistake

Being on the merit list is not enough. You must fill and lock fresh choices in every round, or you are left out of that round.

Round 1

  • Register, pay, lock choices
  • Report with originals
  • Must join to be upgraded later

Round 2

  • New candidates can register
  • Upgrade or keep your seat
  • Old seat is lost if upgraded

Round 3

  • Quota and category conversions
  • Single Girl Child seats
  • Not joining ends your counselling

Stray round

  • No moves between colleges or courses
  • Only private → Govt. upgrade allowed
  • Closed to anyone admitted by Round 3
  • Fill choices even for seats not shown as vacant. Seats free up when others upgrade.
  • Anyone who joins an All India (MCC) seat by Round 3 cannot take part in the State rounds after that.
  • Go to the college only after the final allotment, with the allotment letter downloaded from your login.
  • Refund claims must be made within 6 months of the last admission date.

Watch-outs in this prospectus

These are errors and gaps found in the 22.10.2026 document. Check the AMRU site for corrections.

  1. Copies with a paid-counselling watermark are circulating

    Some copies carry "Send COUNSELLING to WhatsApp 8756077948" on every page. That is not AMRU. Download the original from amruhp.ac.in and use only the official helpline below.

  2. The disability annexure is for MBBS, not PG

    Pages 36–69 and the attached MCC notice are the NMC guidelines for MBBS (UG) 2026 admissions. PwBD candidates should confirm which PG rules apply.

  3. Two different internship deadlines

    Clause 2.1.2 says 30.09.2026. Clause 2.4.3 says "31.09.2026", which is not a real date.

  4. Token amount does not match the fee

    The ₹39,000 Govt. token is called "equal to first-year tuition", but tuition is ₹24,000 and the full college fee is ₹31,500.

  5. MMMC fee period is unclear

    The table does not say per year or full course. MD Pathology at ₹15.95 lakh also stands out against ₹3 lakh for other pre-clinical subjects.

  6. Smaller gaps

    No format or deadline yet for the in-service incentive certificate, Appendix-4 is missing, "Chapter 9" appears twice, and the seat matrix is not published.

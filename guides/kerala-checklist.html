Overview

  • Seats covered: State quota seats in Government Medical Colleges and RCC Thiruvananthapuram, plus all seats in private self-financing colleges (including Minority and NRI quota).
  • Selection: Common counselling by CEE from a state rank list based on NEET-PG 2026 ranks. No separate state exam.
  • Data Sheet: Download date will be announced later on the CEE website.
  • No letters or SMS updates are promised — check cee.kerala.gov.in regularly.

Eligibility

Academic

  • MBBS degree or provisional pass certificate recognised by NMC, with permanent or provisional registration.
  • Internship completed on or before 30/09/2026 Corrected 13/10/2026
  • Kerala State Medical Council registration needed before admission.
The original notification said 30/04/2026. CEE issued a corrigendum on 13 Oct 2026 changing it to 30/09/2026.

Nativity (any one)

  • Indian citizen of Kerala origin.
  • OCI cardholder — treated like Indian citizens for General and NRI seats; no reservation.
  • Non-Keralite who did MBBS in Kerala — no communal / PwD reservation.
  • Non-Keralite service doctors may apply under service quota.

Age

  • No upper age limit, except service quota:
  • Medical Education service quota: 49 years · Health and Insurance Medical service quota: 47 years (as on 31 Dec). Must commit to 10 years' service after the course.

Get ready before you start

The 5 steps

You don't have to finish in one sitting, but all 5 steps must be done before the deadline.

  1. Register — enter NEET roll number, rank and date of birth; add mobile and create a password (8+ characters, with a letter, a number and a symbol). Note down the Application Number — needed through allotment.
  2. Fill application — then click Final Submission. No changes are possible after this.
  3. Pay fee — the "Pay Application Fee" tab turns green when payment succeeds.
  4. Upload photo, signature and certificates — opens only after payment. Images can't be changed after "Finish Upload Image".
  5. Print acknowledgement page — keep it. Do not send it to CEE.
Session timeout: if you stay idle too long, you'll be logged out and unsaved data is lost. Keep documents ready before you begin.

Certificates to upload

PDF only, scanned from originals in colour, 10–500 KB, clear and readable. Several documents for one claim → merge into one PDF (still under 500 KB). One document may be used for more than one claim (e.g. birth certificate for date of birth and nativity).

Mandatory for everyone

  • Proof of nationality
  • Proof of date of birth
  • Proof of nativity

Nativity proof options

  • Kerala origin: SSLC / Indian passport / birth certificate (yours or a parent's, with relationship proof) showing birth in Kerala, or a Village Officer's nativity certificate. Without these: born in Kerala + 5 years' continuous study in Kerala + self-declaration.
  • Non-Keralites with Kerala MBBS: certificate from the college Principal (format on website).

In-service candidates

Send to your Head of Department by 5 PM, 16 Oct 2026:

  • Printout of the application, uploaded documents and acknowledgement page
  • Original declaration that you haven't done a PG degree under service quota
  • Original service details from the Accountant General, Kerala
  • Original service details from the HoD in the prescribed format
  • Date of birth proof

Originals to bring at admission

  • Allotment memo printout
  • Date of birth proof (SSLC / passport / birth certificate)
  • MBBS degree or pass certificate
  • Internship (CRRI) certificate — or, if still in internship in a Kerala college, Principal's certificate confirming completion by 30/09/2026
  • NEET-PG 2026 score sheet
  • Originals of everything uploaded
  • Service candidates: declaration, AG service details, HoD service details

Watch out

  • Internship date: use 30/09/2026 (corrigendum of 13/10/2026), not 30/04/2026.
  • Image file size: official documents say 1–100 KB in one place and 10–100 KB elsewhere. Keep images between 10 and 100 KB to be safe.
  • Old references: the FAQ and How to Apply guide still mention "NEET-PG 2025" / "PG Medical 2025" in places. NEET-PG 2026 is correct.
  • Email: the main notification and payment guide use ceekinfo.cee@kerala.gov.in; the upload guide shows a different address. Use the helpline if unsure.
  • Unofficial copies: some circulating copies of the notification carry a WhatsApp "paid counselling" line on every page. That is not from CEE. Use only official sources.

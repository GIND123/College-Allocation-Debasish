Apply by hand on or before 16 October 2026
  • Write an application on plain paper and attach your supporting documents.
  • Hand it in at the office of the Director of Medical Education, Assam — 2nd floor, Room No. 5, Sixmile, Khanapara, Guwahati-22, during office hours.
  • Post and email applications are not accepted. Nothing is considered after 16 October.
  • Collect your acknowledgement receipt when you submit.

What the notice says

Which seats
The 50% state quota PG seats in Assam's government medical colleges and B.B.C.I., Guwahati, for session 2026.
Rules followed
Medical Colleges of Assam (Regulation of Admission into PG Degree and Diploma Courses) Rules, 2021, as amended.
Who can take part
  1. Eligible under the Assam PG rules
  2. Appeared in NEET-PG 2026 and met the NBE cut-off
  3. Name and roll number appear in this provisional list
Counselling dates
Will follow the MCC (Government of India) schedule. Dates will be posted on dme.assam.gov.in — check it regularly.

CENTAC PG Medical (MD/MS) 2026–27

A plain-language guide to applying through CENTAC for MD/MS seats in Puducherry's four medical colleges, built from the official brochure, the admission notice and the IGMCRI fee notice.

Application deadline
Tuesday 27 Oct 2026, 5:00 PM
Applications opened 20 Oct 2026.
  • Fill details, upload documents and pay before the deadline. An unpaid application is rejected.
  • Tick every quota you might want (GQ and/or MQ). No quota can be added later. ₹5,000 covers both.
  • Only one application per candidate. Duplicates cancel your candidature.

Rules that catch people out

  • Fill fresh preferences every round. Old lists don't carry over.
  • Upgrading cancels your old seat. List only seats you'd truly prefer.
  • Joined an All-India (MCC) seat? You're removed before Round 3.
  • Refunds come only after the whole process, about 3 months later.

Points to clarify with CENTAC

  • The brochure says resigning a Round 1 seat before Round 2 is free, but the Round 3 eligibility table lists it as "with forfeiture".
  • Seat matrix and private-college fee details are still awaited.
  • The brochure and notice refer to "NEET UG" in places; this is the PG process.

Registered candidates should raise queries through the Grievances option in their dashboard. Replies come within 24 hours on working days.

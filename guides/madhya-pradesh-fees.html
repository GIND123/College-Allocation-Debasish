MP PG (MD/MS) Fee Structure

Government and private medical colleges, as per the fee notice dated 25.10.2026. All amounts in ₹.

Read before you decide

  • Other fees = Student fund + Security fund for government colleges (₹11,500; ₹12,500 at Gandhi Medical College Bhopal), and Development & miscellaneous fee for private colleges.
  • Caution money is a refundable deposit (₹3,000 government, ₹25,000 private).
  • The notice does not state whether amounts are per year or for the full course. Confirm with the official MP DME notice before paying.
  • Not listed under NRI means the sheet shows ₹0, which most likely means there is no NRI quota for that seat.
  • ⚑ marks fees that look unusually low compared with the same college's other subjects. These may be data-entry errors, so verify them.
Source: “PG Fee Structure for Govt & Private Institutes”, dated 25.10.2026 · 27 colleges · 410 seat entries.

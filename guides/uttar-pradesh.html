UP NEET PG 2026 Counselling: Rules for Candidates

No. MEd-3/PG-2026/1842 Dated 21 Oct 2026 Issued Lucknow

A plain-English summary of the official Hindi notice for MD, MS, Diploma and DNB counselling. Read the original before acting on anything.

Most common way to lose a seat

An OBC or EWS certificate dated before 1 April 2026, or one in the central-government format. Only the UP state format from a UP authority is accepted.

AWho can take part

  • All
    Only candidates who qualified NEET PG 2026.
  • DNB
    State-quota DNB seats are only for UP in-service candidates.
    Eligibility list released by DG Medical & Health Services on 16 Oct 2026.
  • MD/MS
    In-service weightage applies only to doctors working in the PMHS cadre.
    Same 16 Oct 2026 list.

BChoice filling and allotment

  • Rule
    Seats are allotted by merit. There is no limit on the number of choices.
  • Advice
    Fill only colleges you would actually join.
  • Must
    Lock your choices. Without locking you are left out of allotment, and the responsibility is yours.
  • Rule
    If you get both an MD/MS/Diploma seat and a DNB seat, you can join only one.
  • Money
    Security money is never adjusted against tuition fees.

CWhere you report for admission

Private collegeGovernment-designated nodal centre, named on your allotment letter
Govt MD/MS/DiplomaThe allotted medical college, institute or university
Govt DNBThe allotted medical college or district hospital

DCertificates

  • Date
    OBC and EWS certificates must be issued on or after 1 April 2026.
  • Format
    OBC, SC, ST and EWS certificates must be in the UP government format, issued by a UP competent authority.
  • Format
    Certificates in the format used for central government jobs are not valid.
  • PwD
    Disability certificates must come from centres designated by MCC / DGHS, New Delhi, in the prescribed format.

EAt admission

  • Step
    A medical board examines allotted candidates before admission is completed.
  • Must
    Submit all original academic documents. If any are missing or found incorrect, the seat is cancelled and the security money is forfeited.
  • Warning
    Any forged document, found during or after admission, leads to cancellation and legal action.

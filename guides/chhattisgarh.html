The big 2026 change — who gets which seat

The 22 Jan 2026 amendment replaced Rule 11. In simple terms:

Government colleges

  • 50% of seats go to All India Quota (MCC).
  • The remaining 50% state quota seats are only for doctors who did MBBS from an NMC-recognised college in Chhattisgarh, or CG in-service doctors.
  • If these seats stay vacant, they can be converted for outside (non-CG) graduates in Round 3.

Private colleges

  • 50% Institutional — CG MBBS graduates and CG in-service doctors.
  • 50% Open — allotted on merit to candidates who did not do MBBS in CG.
  • State reservation rules apply to the open seats as well.
Practical meaning: if your MBBS is from outside Chhattisgarh, in Rounds 1–2 you can only compete for private "Open" seats (and NRI seats, if eligible).

Basic requirements

  • Indian citizen with an MBBS from an NMC/MCI-recognised college (or FMGE-qualified).
  • Internship completed by the date set by the Central Government.
  • Registered with NMC or a State Medical Council.

You are not eligible if you

  • Left (resigned) an AIQ/State PG seat after joining, or were expelled — barred for 3 years.
  • Completed a PG degree in the last 3 years.
  • Took a PG seat through All India Quota in the last 3 years (not eligible for CG state quota).
  • Have a CG MBBS rural-service bond but no NOC from the Health Commissioner.
EWS: you get EWS benefit only if you chose EWS in your NEET-PG 2026 form. No change is allowed now.

Bonus marks for in-service doctors

  • 10% of your NEET score for each full year in a notified difficult/remote scheduled area.
  • 3% for each full year in a general scheduled area.
  • Maximum 30%. You need at least 3 years of CG government service by the NEET-PG exam date.
  • Uploading the service certificate is mandatory for in-service candidates.

Service bond (Government college seats)

  • Applies to everyone admitted to a CG government college — state quota and AIQ.
  • Must work 2 years for CG Health & Family Welfare after PG.
  • Bond amount: ₹50 lakh (unreserved) / ₹40 lakh (reserved).
  • Leaving the seat after the national last date: ₹25 lakh (unreserved) / ₹20 lakh (reserved).
  • The bond automatically ends if no posting order comes within 12 months of the university result.
  • Bonds are on ₹250 non-judicial stamp paper, notarised, with a surety and two witnesses.

NRI quota (private colleges only)

Deadline: 27 Oct 2026, 5:00 PM — earlier than the registration deadline.
  • Email all documents to cgneetugnri2026@gmail.com as one single PDF, colour scans, in the order listed below.
  • Subject line: NEET PG 2026 – NRI Quota Document Verification – Candidate Name – NEET Roll Number
  • Emailing is not registration. You must still register online, pay ₹10,000 and fill choices.
  • Only those on the Eligible list get NRI seats. Ineligible candidates continue in their normal category.
  • OCI cardholders can apply for NRI seats under the general category.

Documents

  1. Family tree certificate (Tehsildar or higher) proving blood relation with the NRI sponsor — parent, sibling, sibling's child, uncle/aunt (both sides) or their children, grandparent.
  2. Proof the sponsor lives abroad — green card / passport with valid visa / citizenship document / proof of 181+ days of work.
  3. NRE/NRI bank account statement.
  4. Sponsor's income proof (able to pay the fee).
  5. OCI card (if applicable).
  6. Guardian-based claim: affidavit, proof of care, and legal guardianship under the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890.

Fees must be paid in foreign currency by the sponsor; the receipt or bank-attested statement must reach the Commissioner's office within 7 days of admission.

Roster continues from last year's roster point for each college, so the category of each seat can differ year to year.

Documents for scrutiny (originals + 2 photocopy sets)

  1. NEET-PG admit card and score card
  2. All MBBS marksheets with internship certificate, or MBBS degree
  3. Permanent registration (CG Medical Council registration within 3 months if from another state)
  4. Domicile (genuine resident) certificate — if required
  5. CG permanent caste certificate; OBC non-creamy layer income proof — if applicable
  6. PwBD certificate from CG State Medical Board — if applicable
  7. EWS certificate issued on/after 1 April of the admission year — if applicable
  8. Service certificate (in-service candidates)
  9. Migration certificate (within 3 months if university changes)
  10. Medical fitness certificate (District Medical Board / medical college)
  11. Service bond and seat-leaving bond
  12. Aadhaar or other photo ID; 3 identical passport photos
  13. Private colleges: bank guarantee for 1 year's fee
Certificates must be issued at least one day before your scrutiny date. Being born in CG, studying there, or having a parent in CG government service is not enough — you need the actual domicile certificate.

Watch-outs

  • Two deadlines on 27 Oct: NRI documents at 5 PM, registration at 11:59 PM.
  • Non-CG graduates cannot get government state-quota seats in Rounds 1–2.
  • Round 1 is the only free exit. From Round 2, not joining costs the deposit — up to ₹2 lakh for private seats.
  • Government seats carry a ₹40–50 lakh bond, including AIQ seats in CG.
  • Guardian-based NRI claims now need legal guardianship under the 1890 Act — hard to arrange at short notice.
  • Use official sources only. Circulating copies carry third-party WhatsApp "paid counselling" watermarks — these are not from the government. Download from the official sites.

Karnataka PG Medical Counselling 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

For MD / MS / Diploma seats in Karnataka, and DNB & Diploma seats for Karnataka in-service doctors. Based on KEA's PGET-2026 notification and the PG Medical & Dental Information Bulletin 2026.

Steps to follow

  1. 1

    Check you are eligible

    50th percentile or above in NEET-PG 2026. Non-Karnataka candidates must meet the General (UR) cut-off. Anyone already holding a clinical PG degree cannot apply.

  2. 2

    Read the Information Bulletin

    It covers eligibility clauses, seat types, documents and allotment rules.

    cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/KEA/pgetmed2026
  3. 3

    Create your profile

    Enter your NEET-PG roll number. Name, parents' names, date of birth, score and mobile number come from NBE data. An OTP goes to the mobile number you gave NBE.

  4. 4

    Fill the form

    Choose your eligibility clause code, reservation category, education details and in-service status. Upload photo, left thumb impression and signature.

  5. 5

    Check everything, then declare

    You can edit any number of times before the declaration. After declaring, nothing can be changed, including category and income.

  6. 6

    Pay the fee and print the form

    Pay online through the payment gateway. Save your Application ID / Login ID and keep the printout until counselling ends.

  7. 7

    Book a slot and attend verification

    Book on the KEA website (Annexure 18–19). Go in person to KEA, 18th Cross, Malleswaram, Bengaluru with originals. Collect your document verification slip. Without it you cannot enter options.

  8. 8

    Enter options and check mock allotment

    Options are entered once and carry through the rounds. A mock allotment lets you reorder, add or delete options before the real one.

  9. 9

    Act on your allotment

    After round 1, pick one of four choices. After later rounds you must join the allotted college. See Rounds.

Seat types

G · Government

50% of seats in government colleges (other 50% go to All India Quota via MCC), 33% in private non-minority, 20% in private minority, and 40% in private university colleges.

P · Private

42% in KPCF colleges, split equally into GMP (Karnataka merit) and OPN (all-India, Indian citizens). 55% in KRLMPCA / AMPCK colleges: 66% for the minority group, the rest split GMP / OPN. 40% in private universities.

N · NRI

15% of seats in KPCF, KRLMPCA, AMPCK colleges and private universities. For NRI, NRI wards, PIO, OCI and foreign nationals.

Q · Others

10% in KPCF, KRLMPCA and AMPCK colleges, 5% in private universities. Open to all NEET-PG qualified candidates except NRI / PIO / OCI / foreign nationals.

Minority seat codes: MC (Christian), MM (Muslim), MA (Tamil), ME (Telugu), MK (Kodava), MU (Tulu). Linguistic minority needs Tamil, Telugu, Kodava or Tulu as mother tongue plus 10 years of study in Karnataka. St. John's Medical College has its own four categories.

You choose one clause code in the form. It decides which seats you can take and which documents you must show.

OPN and Q seats are open to any Indian citizen with an MBBS, whatever their clause. Only natural-born children count (not adopted children or grandchildren). Repeating a class counts as one year. Foreign medical graduates must have passed FMGE.

Documents for verification

Carry originals plus one set of photocopies. Mandatory for everyone:

  • KEA PGET-2026 application printout
  • Photo ID with address (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID, driving licence or ration card)
  • NEET-PG 2026 admit card and scorecard
  • SSLC / 10th marks card (date of birth proof)
  • 2nd PUC / 12th marks card
  • MBBS marks cards of all phases
  • MBBS degree certificate (provisional degree certificate only if passed in 2026)
  • Internship completion certificate (Annexure 5)
  • Certificate from head of institution that your college is NMC-recognised
  • Permanent medical council registration (provisional accepted only if passed in 2026)
  • Undertaking in Annexure 7, on white paper and on ₹100 e-stamp paper
  • 371(J) Kalyana Karnataka certificate (Annexure 8), if claiming that reservation
Extra documents by clause and category
  • Clause b, c: 10-year study certificate in Annexure 11, countersigned by BEO / DDPI, plus SSLC or 2nd PUC marks card from Karnataka.
  • Clause d: DPAR certificate showing the parent's All India Service cadre and period served outside Karnataka.
  • Clause e: Parent's 10-year study certificate (Annexure 11) and employer's home-town declaration showing transferable service.
  • Clause f: Parliament Secretariat certificate and the candidate's 10-year study certificate.
  • Clause g: Parent's home-town declaration from the defence records office, or from Sainik Welfare for ex-servicemen.
  • Clause z: Passport / visa, parents' residence proof from the embassy, citizenship proof, income tax documents.
  • Reservation: Caste / income certificate from the Tahsildar. SC/ST in Form-D, Category-1 in Form-E, 2A/2B/3A/3B in Form-F. Must be in the name of candidate and father (married women: with husband's name and income).
  • Religious minority: Tahsildar certificate (Annexure 11 & 12).
  • Linguistic minority: Certificate from BEO / DDPI (Annexure 9 & 10).
  • NRI ward: Annexure 17 and 17(A), with an embassy certificate of the sponsor issued within 6 months.

Submit at the college after admission: RGUHS eligibility certificate and migration certificate (if MBBS was not from RGUHS).

Karnataka SC candidates: Your caste certificate must show the internal sub-group SCA, SCB or SCC, and you must enter its RD number. Without it you cannot take SC-reserved seats.

Counselling rounds

Allotment goes by NEET rank, your option order and the government roster. There are three rounds plus a stray vacancy round, all online through KEA.

After Round 1: pick one of four choices

Choice 1

Fully happy with the seat. Pay fees, submit originals, collect the admission order, join and update joining status on the KEA portal.

Out of all later rounds.

Choice 2

Keep this seat but try for a better one. Pay fees and deposit originals. Your current seat becomes your last option.

If upgraded, the old seat is lost automatically.

Choice 3

Give up this seat and try again in Round 2. No fee to pay, but deposit originals.

No claim on the surrendered seat.

Choice 4

Quit KEA counselling (for example, you got a seat elsewhere).

Seat cancelled, out of all later rounds.

Not picking any choice by the deadline cancels the seat and removes you from later rounds.

Round 2

  • Open to Choice 2 and Choice 3 candidates, and those with no Round 1 seat, who deposited originals.
  • You can reorder or delete options but cannot add new ones.
  • No choices after the result. If allotted, you must pay and join.

Round 3 (Mop-up)

  • Open to those with no seat after Round 2, and to those who joined a KEA-allotted college (for upgrade).
  • Fresh option entry. Earlier options are not carried over.
  • Not open to those who joined a seat through MCC / deemed university / other boards.
  • A new seat here cancels your earlier seat with no way back.

Stray vacancy round

  • Only for candidates who never got a KEA PG seat in any round and have not joined a seat elsewhere.

Bonds, service and penalties

1-year compulsory service

After PG, everyone (except those who already did rural service after MBBS) serves one year in a government hospital. Only temporary registration until then. Bond on ₹100 stamp paper (Annexure 1).

3-year government service

Government seat holders (including AIQ) sign an undertaking on ₹200 stamp paper (Annexure 2) to serve 3 years if asked. Breaking it: ₹50 lakh (degree) or ₹25 lakh (diploma).

When you cancel or leaveDegreeDiploma

Lower penalties apply to pre-clinical (₹1 lakh) and para-clinical (₹2 lakh degree, ₹75,000 diploma) seats. Fees paid are forfeited. Leaving after the last admission date also bars you from any PG seat for 3 years, and you must repay the full course fee and stipend.

Fee concessions

  • Karnataka SC/ST, income below ₹2.5 lakh: no tuition fee on government seats.
  • Karnataka SC/ST, income ₹2.5–10 lakh: pay 50% of tuition fee on government seats.
  • Category-1, income below ₹2.5 lakh: no fee in government colleges.
  • OBC: pay the fee, then claim reimbursement from the Backward Class Department.
  • Private seats: full fee. In-service candidates pay full fee and claim from their department. NRI and Q seats need a bank guarantee.

Important

  • Qualifying NEET-PG alone does not give you a seat. You must complete registration and document verification.
  • Details entered before the declaration are final. KEA will not change them later.
  • Once a seat is allotted from your options, you cannot refuse it. Only enter colleges you will actually join, after checking the fee.
  • Not reporting to an allotted college counts as seat blocking. It attracts penalties and possible legal action.
  • PwD reservation is for Karnataka candidates only, with a compulsory medical exam at KEA.
  • Ignore touts promising seats. Report them to keauthority-ka@nic.in. Trust only notices on the KEA website, not WhatsApp numbers added to copies of notices.

Round 1 dates

    Registration fee: ₹8,000 for every category. Round 2 and 3 dates will be announced later; fresh registration may be allowed in Round 2.

    Who can apply

    State quota seats

    • Indian citizen
    • MBBS from a medical college in Uttarakhand
    • Qualified NEET PG 2026
    • Internship finished by 30 Sep 2026 — no exceptions
    • NMC / State Medical Council registration

    All-India open quota (private colleges only)

    • MBBS from any NMC-recognised college
    • Same NEET, internship and registration rules
    • No caste reservation on these seats
    • State-quota candidates can apply here too
    • Not eligible: anyone currently doing a PG course in Uttarakhand, until they finish it.
    • In-service PMHS doctors: separate Diploma / DNB seats at Srinagar, with NOC from DG Health.
    • NRI quota: 15% of SGRR's all-India seats, per the bulletin.
    • Domicile certificate is needed to claim reservation benefits.

    The government service bond

    At government colleges, clinical MD/MS students choose with bond or without bond.

    With bond

    ₹60,000 / yr

    ≈ ₹2.5 L total for 3 years

    Without bond

    ₹5,00,000 / yr

    ≈ ₹15.7 L total for 3 years

    What you promise with the bond: after the course, serve 2 years in a government hospital or health centre in a hilly area anywhere in Uttarakhand — or pay ₹2.5 crore. Only take the bonded rate if you're genuinely willing to serve. (Source: GO 374 of 2017, restated in a 2021 government letter.)
    • Non-clinical branches (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology, Forensic Medicine, Microbiology, Community Medicine): no bond, ₹1 L / yr since 2020.
    • Pathology is the odd one out: no bond option and not part of the 2020 fee cut, so it costs ₹5 L / yr.
    • Private colleges have their own bonds: GBCM — ₹20 L for one year of senior residency at GBCM or in a hilly area. HIMS mentions a bond but doesn't explain it.

    How allotment works

    • Round 1 is a free exit. Don't like your seat? Skip it — no penalty, and you stay in for later rounds.
    • Fill as many choices as you like, in your real order of preference. Once a seat is allotted it won't be changed.
    • After allotment you must pick one option before the joining deadline:
      • Freeze — happy with the seat. Go to the college in person with original documents and pay fees.
      • Float — keep the seat but try for a better one next round. Documents are checked online; no need to go.
    • Pick neither and the seat is cancelled (you can still join Round 2).
    • Leaving after joining: refund minus ₹6,000 plus any hostel/mess used — only if the board permits.
    • False information = permanent disqualification, even if found years later.

    Within each category: women 30%, defence wards 5%, orphans 5%, PwBD 4%, freedom fighters' dependants 2%.

    • OBC certificate must be less than 3 years old.
    • PwBD certificate only from an NMC-designated medical board.
    • Missing original caste or domicile certificate at admission = treated as general category.
    • Private colleges also keep 25–30% seats for Uttarakhand permanent residents (GBCM 25%, HIMS 30%).

    Documents to keep ready

    • Class 10 certificate (age proof) and Class 12 marksheet
    • NEET PG 2026 admit card and scorecard
    • MBBS degree and internship completion certificate
    • NMC / State Medical Council registration
    • Category / sub-category / PwBD certificate (if claiming)
    • Uttarakhand domicile certificate (Uttarakhand candidates)
    • ID proof — Aadhaar preferred
    • NOC — for in-service or bonded candidates
    • Allotment letter printout
    • Fee payment (online advance + demand draft for the rest)

    Upload clear scans within 2–3 days of registering. Blurred or mismatched documents are the most common reason for queries or cancellation.

    Things to double-check

    Gaps and contradictions in the official documents. Ask the board or college before you rely on them.

    1. Registration fee validity: bulletin says "two rounds (I, II & III)" — unclear if Round 3 needs a new fee, and whether the ₹8,000 is refundable.
    2. Refund deduction: bulletin says ₹6,000; Doon's fee sheet says ₹10,000 for non-bonded seats.
    3. Payment method: bulletin says online advance tuition; Doon asks for a demand draft plus ₹1,000 cash.
    4. Residents' quota at private colleges: GBCM says 25% of seats, HIMS says 30% — both quote the same law. The rebate is given "after admission", so expect to pay the full amount first.
    5. What "state quota" means: the bulletin bases it on MBBS from Uttarakhand; private colleges tie their rebate to permanent residence.
    6. NRI seats: the bulletin names only SGRR, yet GBCM has published NRI fees. SGRR's NRI table is labelled 2025-26 and uses an old dollar rate.
    7. HIMS: no mess fee listed (expect ~₹2 L / yr extra); security deposit tied to an unexplained bond.
    8. Seat lists missing: Haldwani mentions seats "under compliance" (conditional NMC permission) but the list wasn't attached.
    9. Bond penalty: the ₹2.5 crore figure dates from 2017 — confirm it's current.
    10. Srinagar and Almora: full fee sheets (hostel etc.) not published with these documents.
    11. Bank transfers: verify any account number directly with the college before sending money. Fake fee notices circulate every year.

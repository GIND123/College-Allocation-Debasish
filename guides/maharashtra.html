Are you eligible?

Your eligibility is judged on the last day of registration. Becoming eligible after that day does not count.

The deposit decision

The single most important choice at registration. The amount you pay decides which colleges you can ever choose.

Deposit optionOpen categoryReserved categoryColleges you can choose
Government only₹25,000₹12,500Government, corporation and government-aided only
Government + private₹1,00,000₹1,00,000All colleges, including private and minority (S, I and N seats)
It cannot be changed later.

No extension is given for the deposit after registration closes. If there is any chance you would accept a private seat, pay ₹1,00,000. This is on top of the non-refundable ₹3,000 registration fee.

When you get it back, and when you lose it

Refunded (to the same account / card)

  • You joined a seat and kept it to the end, or
  • You never got a seat in the whole process.

Refunds happen at the end of the entire admission process.

Forfeited

  • You don't join a seat allotted in Round 2 or later.
  • You resign after joining in Round 2 or later (including after filling a Status Retention Form).
  • Your admission is cancelled for false information or missing documents.

If forfeited after Round 2, you must pay the fee and deposit again to join Round 3.

Category and reservation rules

Claim your category at registration, with documents uploaded by the deadline.

You cannot switch from Open to a reserved category after submitting. If any category document is missing, you are treated as Open (if you meet the Open percentile).

Seat types and choice codes

Each choice is a 4-digit code plus a letter. The first two digits are the college, the next two the subject.

S State quota

All seats in government colleges, and 50% of seats in private and minority colleges.

I Institutional quota

35% of private-college seats, filled on all-India merit (out-of-state graduates, FMGs and others).

N NRI quota

15% of private-college seats. Unfilled NRI seats go by merit to anyone who listed N codes.

Each letter has a different fee.

For the same college and subject, list S first, then I, then N. Check private-college fees at mahafra.org before filling choices.

Private health sciences universities reserve 40% of seats for Maharashtra domicile; their fees are set by the university and students there get no government scholarships.

How the rounds work

Round 1 → Round 2 → Round 3 → online stray vacancy round(s). There is no college-level stray round.

Two rules for every round

1. Fill choices fresh each round — old choices are not carried forward (except between stray rounds).
2. Once you click Submit on your choices, they are locked for that round.

Round 1 — the safe round

  • Not joining = free exit. You stay in Round 2 without registering again.
  • Joined but want better? Don't fill the Status Retention Form; fill new choices in Round 2.
  • Happy with the seat? Fill the Status Retention Form and get a written acknowledgement. You leave all later rounds.
  • You can still resign before the stated date and keep free exit.

Round 2 — commitments begin

  • If you get an upgrade, you must move to it.
  • No upgrade? You keep your Round 1 seat, but lose free exit.
  • Not joining a Round 2 seat = deposit lost and out of the process (re-register and pay again for Round 3).

Round 3

  • New registrations open again before Round 3.
  • A seat allotted here is your last — no further rounds.
  • Anyone holding an All India Quota seat (up to AIQ Round 3) can't take part.

Stray vacancy round(s)

  • Only for registered candidates without a seat after Round 3.
  • No new registration.
  • Not joining = deposit lost.
The Status Retention Form is permanent.

It cannot be withdrawn, and the deadline for it is never extended.

Joining is in person — no proxy. Bring all originals and fees. A change of category alone (for example reserved to open in the same seat) does not count as an upgrade.

Fees, refunds and penalties

Government and corporation colleges

  • Tuition: ₹1,67,300 per year
  • Development fee: ₹5,000
  • Admission ₹1,500 · Gymkhana ₹500 · Hostel ₹4,000 per year

Private and minority colleges

Set by the Fee Regulating Authority — different for S, I and N seats. See mahafra.org. Private colleges can't demand a bank guarantee or a service bond.

Central institutes

Tata Memorial, BARC, MGIMS Sevagram and AIIPMR have their own fees and lapse penalties of ₹10–20 lakh plus bonds. See brochure page 37.

How to pay at joining

By demand draft — one DD for tuition and one for other fees, in favour of the Dean / Principal. A cheque is accepted only on a bank holiday and must be swapped for a DD the next working day, or the admission can be cancelled.

Cancelling

  • Before the prescribed date: ₹1,500 is deducted and the rest refunded.
  • After the prescribed date: no refund.

NRI candidates

From Notice 02. NRIs are eligible only for the 15% NRI seats in private and minority colleges.

Who counts as NRI

  • As defined in the Income Tax Act, 2025, with an NRI certificate from an Indian Mission or Post abroad.
  • The candidate, the candidate's parent, or a ward under the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890.

Steps

  1. Register and upload documents on the Foreign Candidate Registration portal, fn.mahacet.org, before 22 Oct.
  2. Pay the USD 50 eligibility fee (non-refundable).
  3. If eligible, receive a Provisional Eligibility Certificate (PEC) by email and SMS. It can also support a visa application.
  4. Pay the USD 1,150 processing fee. Missing either payment drops you from the process.
  5. Log in to the regular CAP portal with your FCR application number and complete registration, payment and choices like everyone else.

Documents

  • NRI status certificate from an Indian Mission or Post
  • Passport and nationality certificate of the candidate and of the parent / guardian
  • Court guardianship order, if claiming as a ward
  • Foreign bank passbook copy with name and address, showing the last 6 months of entries (counted back from the date of admission)
  • FCR portal receipt
Pay the higher deposit.

NRI seats exist only in private colleges, so NRI candidates will almost certainly need the ₹1,00,000 deposit as well as the ₹3,000 fee. The notice doesn't say this directly — confirm with the help desk.

Getting an APAAR ID

APAAR (also called the ABC ID, Academic Bank of Credits) is a permanent government student ID. The CET Cell published a guide on 20 Oct. The brochure doesn't say it is compulsory for NEET-PG, but create one if the form asks for it.

  1. Go to digilocker.gov.in and click Login / Register.
  2. Log in with your mobile or Aadhaar number and your 6-digit DigiLocker PIN.
  3. Click “Get Now” for APAAR ID, or search “APAAR” and choose Academic Bank of Credits.
  4. Click APAAR ID. Name, date of birth and gender fill in from Aadhaar.
  5. Enter your roll, registration or enrolment number and choose its type. Graduates can try their MUHS enrolment number.
  6. Pick your admission year and college.
  7. Tick consent and click Get Document. Your APAAR ID is created and saved in DigiLocker.
No enrolment number?

Choose “None” at step 5, then tick consent and click Get Document. Make sure your Aadhaar name and date of birth match your MBBS and NEET-PG records.

Raising a help-desk ticket

For problems like a payment that didn't update, an upload error or a wrong category. From the CET Cell guide of 20 Oct.

  1. Go to cetcell.mahacet.org and open “CET (Examination) portal for A.Y. 2026-27”.
  2. Sign in with your registered email and password.
  3. Click Help Desk, then Open a New Query.
  4. Choose the closest Help Topic, and write a 4–5 word title, e.g. “Deposit paid, status not updated”.
  5. Category: CAP. Pick the course (or Other); the department should become Medical Education.
  6. Enter your CAP application number if you have one, then choose “CAP Enquiry” and the closest sub-category.
  7. Describe the problem in full — what, when, and any transaction or application numbers.
  8. Attach screenshots or receipts as proof and click Create Ticket.

Track it: Help Desk → Track Your Query → click your ticket number.

A ticket does not pause any deadline.

Keep paying and uploading on time. Merit-list corrections go through the formal grievance window (29–30 Oct) with proof. If your NEET-PG login doesn't work on this portal, email cetcell@mahacet.org.

Common traps

Paying the smaller deposit

It permanently shuts you out of every private, institutional and NRI seat.

Uploading but not submitting

Click Submit after the last upload, or the form is rejected.

Wrong EWS format

Only the Maharashtra state format for 2026–27 is accepted.

Expired non-creamy layer

It must be valid until 31 Mar 2027 or later.

Picking the wrong suffix

An I or N seat can cost far more than the S seat in the same college.

Filling SRF too early

Once submitted it can't be withdrawn, and you leave all later rounds.

Skipping a Round 2 seat

You lose the deposit and must re-register to continue.

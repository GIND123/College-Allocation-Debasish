3Who can apply

  • MBBS from an NMC-recognised college.
  • Permanent resident of Odisha, with the online resident certificate that has a QR code.
    Exception: an out-of-state student who did MBBS in an Odisha Govt college through the 15% All India Quota.
  • Qualified NEET-PG 2026 with the NMC cut-off score.
  • Internship completed by 30 Sep 2026, or the date MCC/NBE notifies.
  • Registered with a State or Central Medical Council.
  • If you are already in a PG course, you must pay the bond penalty before taking a new seat.

Direct or in-service?

TypeWho
DirectUnemployed, or working in Odisha Govt / PSU / Defence in Odisha but less than 3 years of service by 31 March 2026.
In-serviceAt least 3 years of service by 31 March 2026, counting contractual, ad-hoc and regular work. Any single leave of 30 days or more is not counted, except maternity leave.
Diploma (state quota)Only for candidates in regular Govt / PSU / Defence service in Odisha.

4Seats & reservation

Main categories (vertical)

22.5%Scheduled Tribe (ST)
16.25%Scheduled Caste (SC)
11.25%SEBC

Within each category (horizontal)

  • 5% Persons with Disabilities (PwD)
  • 3% Ex-servicemen (ExS)
  • 5% Green Card holders (GC)

Other points

  • EWS applies to General candidates only, and only in subjects where extra EWS seats were added. Unfilled EWS seats go to General.
  • ST, SC and SEBC candidates cannot take EWS seats.
  • Central-list OBC (non-creamy layer) candidates may apply but are placed in UR.
  • Management quota seats in private colleges are UR only, with no reservation.
  • ST and SC seats cannot be swapped with each other.

Category certificate notes

  • Green Card: the parent's original card is needed. It must not have been used for reservation before, and no affidavit is accepted for mismatches.
  • Ex-servicemen: a certificate from the Rajya Sainik Board, Bhubaneswar. You are not eligible if you or your parent is still serving.
  • EWS: family income under ₹8 lakh, at most 5 acres of farmland, flat under 1,000 sq ft, and plot limits as per the certificate rules.
  • PwD: the certificate must come from an MCC-notified centre only. See Watch-outs.

5In-service bonus marks

In-service candidates get extra marks for days worked in remote or difficult areas, counted up to 31 March 2026. The bonus is capped at 30% of their NEET-PG score.

  • Diploma applicants get no bonus marks.

Service Certificate (in-service candidates)

Your in-service status and bonus marks are checked against this certificate. It must be based on your Service Book and carry the officer's signature, name and seal.

Who should sign it? The form itself says "Head of Office". The Convener's document guidelines (10.10.2026) say CDM & PHO or the Superintendent of your institution, and call it the "Service Continuity & Area Posting Certificate". To be safe, get it signed by the CDM & PHO or Superintendent.
PartWhat it must show
A. Institutions servedEvery institution you worked in, with:
• name of the institution
• block and district
• type of service (ad-hoc, temporary, contractual or regular)
• date of joining and date of relief
B. Long leaveAny single leave of 30 days or more, not counting maternity leave, with:
• institution, block and district
• type of service
• date you went on leave and date you rejoined
  • Part A decides your bonus marks. The name, block and district of each institution should match how they appear in the area lists (Annexure A, B and C), so they can be matched to the right grade.
  • Part B decides your 3-year service count. Those leave days are taken out of your service period.
  • The certificate is valid for MD/MS, DNB and Post-MBBS NBEMS Diploma counselling for 2026-27.
Don't mix up the names. This form is titled "Annexure-A", but in the main guidelines "Annexure A" is the list of 10% bonus areas. They are two different documents.
Tip: if you served in more than one place, check that every posting is listed with exact dates, with no gaps or overlaps. Get the certificate early, because offices can be slow.

6How counselling works

Before the rounds

  • Register on the candidate login page by creating a username and password. Without registration you can't move forward.
  • Fill the online form, submit it and print it.
  • Pay the ₹2,500 fee via SBI Collect. It is not refunded.
  • Attend document verification. For 2026-27 it is in person at MKCG Medical College, Berhampur (see Key dates). An authorised person may go for you in an emergency. If you miss it, your name will not appear in the merit list.

The four rounds

Online
Round 1

Lock your choices of college and course, in any number. Seats are allotted on merit and choice.

Online
Round 2

Choose Float or Freeze. Vacated seats are re-allotted. After this round, you can't resign to join All India counselling.

Physical
Mop-up

Seats are allotted on the spot in a fixed category order. Bring your original CLC and the fee. Diploma seats come after MD/MS.

Physical
Stray vacancy

All unfilled seats become General. Only candidates who have not joined any seat can take part.

Rounds may all move online in an emergency. Dates follow the MCC and Supreme Court schedule.

7Mop-up round seat order (Addendum, 23 Sep 2026)

Step 1: In-service seats are filled first, then Direct seats.
Step 2: Within each, the order below is followed from top to bottom.

ST
ST (PwD)→ST (GC)→ST (ExS)→ST
SC
SC (PwD)→SC (GC)→SC (ExS)→SC
SEBC
SEBC (PwD)→SEBC (GC)→SEBC (ExS)→SEBC
UR
UR (PwD)→UR (GC)→UR (ExS)→UR (EWS)→UR

What happens to empty seats

  • An empty sub-category seat goes back to its main category. For example, an empty SC (PwD) seat becomes a regular SC seat.
  • An empty in-service seat goes to Direct candidates and keeps its reservation.
  • An empty reserved seat is finally filled by UR candidates.

Mop-up rules

  • Once you are admitted in mop-up, you can't change your seat or subject in the same round.
  • You can take part only in the slot you are called for. If you come late, you choose only from seats still left.
  • You can't resign an MD/MS seat to try for a Diploma seat.

Stray vacancy order

In-service general→Direct general→Diploma general

8Strict rules & penalties

₹10 lakh penalty, plus all stipend or salary received, if you resign after mop-up or don't report to the college in time. The admission fee is also forfeited.
  • Surrender, when allowed under MCC rules: you get back 90% of the admission fee, and 10% is kept.
  • If you hold a seat at the end of Round 2, you can't join later rounds of state or All India counselling.
  • No new registration after the first round, unless the cut-off is lowered or new seats are added.
  • False or hidden information means cancellation, possible legal action and a 3-year ban.
  • For Govt seats, a service bond with sureties must be submitted at admission. This does not apply to private colleges.
  • Leave is 15 days a year, with at most 10 days at a stretch. Extra days must be made up. Attendance must be at least 80%.
  • Private practice during PG is not allowed, and is grounds for cancellation.
  • Ragging leads to expulsion if the explanation is not satisfactory.

10Watch-outs

No disability certificate centre in Odisha. PwD candidates must go to an MCC-notified centre. The nearest is IPGMER, Kolkata. Plan early.
Two rules may conflict. The addendum says empty in-service seats go to Direct candidates with their reservation intact. The older clause 13.4, which was not removed, says empty in-service reserved seats first go to in-service UR. Watch for the convener's notice on this.
Order only set for mop-up. The addendum's category order covers the mop-up round only. No order is stated for Rounds 1 and 2.
Two fee rates at DRIEMS. DRIEMS now has separate state quota and management quota fees, and clinical management seats cost ₹38.43 lakh a year. The Hi-Tech fees in the guidelines don't show a split like this. Ask the college if you are unsure which rate applies to your seat.
Check your area's grade carefully. The area lists for bonus marks (Annexure A/B) contain duplicates and spelling variations. Verify your exact institution name and grade.
Transport fee gap. The Hi-Tech transport fee is set for distances up to 12 km and over 20 km. Ask the college about 12–20 km.

11Valid documents

As per the Convener's "Guidelines for valid documents", No. 35/MCB-2026, dated 10.10.2026.

Illegible or handwritten copies are rejected. Certificates that are meant to be generated online must carry their QR code or barcode, or they are not valid.

Required from everyone

DocumentWhat makes it valid
College Leaving / Transfer CertificateOriginal paper copy. Required from every candidate who is allotted a seat.
NEET-PG scorecardA printout of the PDF from the official portal, for this year. It must show qualifying marks as per NMC.
MBBS degree & mark sheetsClear, high-resolution copies. A permanent degree or a provisional one issued by the university is accepted. The college must be NMC/MCI recognised.
Internship certificateIssued by the Dean/Principal. It must show completion on or before 30 Sep 2026, or the date NBEMS notifies.
Medical registrationA clear printout of the digital copy from OMC or NMC/MCI. It must be valid on the day you register online.

Required if it applies to you

DocumentWhat makes it valid
Permanent Resident CertificateThe online version with QR code. It must be valid on the day you lock choices. Not needed by out-of-state AIQ graduates of Odisha Govt colleges.
ST / SC certificateThe online version with QR code or barcode, under Odisha Caste Rules. Issued by a Tahsildar or higher. Valid permanently unless revoked.
SEBC-NCL certificateGenerated online through the e-District portal and showing local Non-Creamy Layer status. It must be issued on or after 1 April 2026 and is valid for 1 year.
EWS certificateThe online version, issued by a Tahsildar or higher. It must be issued on or after 1 April 2026 and is valid for 1 year.
PwD certificateThe format set under the RPwD Act 2016, valid for this year. It must come only from a DGHS/MCC-notified medical board.
Ex-Servicemen certificateIssued by the Rajya Sainik Board, Odisha, on or after 1 April 2026. You are not eligible if you or a parent is still serving.
Green CardYour parent's original card, not used for reservation before. It must match your other documents, and no affidavit is accepted. If your name, parents' names or date of birth don't match, get a corrected card from the CDM & PHO. Otherwise you will be treated as UR.
Service Continuity & Area Posting CertificateRequired from all in-service candidates, in the set format. It must be signed by the CDM & PHO or the Superintendent of your institution, and must list any leave of 30 days or more.
Indemnity BondRequired from everyone allotted a seat, except for private college seats. It goes on non-judicial stamp paper worth at least ₹21, signed by you and two sureties.
Problem with a document? Email the Convener at convenerpgmedicalcounselling26@gmail.com. Only complaints sent to this address are considered.

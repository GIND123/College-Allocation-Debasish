Sources, definitions and data checks

Supplied provisional matrices

All three documents are dated 26 October 2026, issued by the PG (Medical) Counselling Committee, M.K.C.G. Medical College, Brahmapur. Each source has one page.

    2025 comparison sources

      The 2025 data has 238 non-zero allocation records, totalling 426 seats. All 100 independently transcribed course, institution and pool controls reconcile. The reservation breakdown is transcribed for all 238 records and reconciles with all 426 printed seats: UR 295, EWS 9, SC 29, ST 44, and 49 standalone special-category seats (PH 19, GC 19, ES 11). The latter have an Unspecified base category; PH maps to PwD for filtering. No SEBC allocation is printed in the 2025 images. Combining a specific base category with a standalone special category is n.a. where that relationship is not stated. AHPGIC (2025) is matched to AHRCC (2026), following the institution rename described on the official AHPGIC website. SJ MCH, BB MCH, SLN, RGH and the other explicit college/hospital abbreviations are matched to their corresponding 2026 labels. Zero in a comparison means no seat is listed in that supplied snapshot.

      Government / private classification

      Institution type describes the college or hospital ownership, independently of its seat quota or pool. Hi-Tech Bhubaneswar, Hi-Tech Rourkela and DRIEMS are Private. The government medical colleges, PGIMER / Capital Hospital, AHRCC and listed district / government hospitals are Government. A regular state-quota seat at a private institution remains classified as Private. This classification applies to both supplied years.

        Source discrepancies retained

        S1 · MKCG · In-service · Community Medicine: 3 seats printed. Category text: 1-ST + 1-SC + 2-UR = 4.

        S1 · MKCG · In-service · General Medicine: 4 seats printed. Category text: 1-ST + 1-SC + 1-UR = 3.

        Counting. The full data set contains 524 seats: 463 MD/MS (386 regular + 77 management), 43 diploma and 18 DNB. All 160 row, institution and pool control checks reconcile. The two category differences offset overall but remain unresolved at cell level.

        Reservation and additional categories. UR = Unreserved; EWS = Economically Weaker Section; SC = Scheduled Caste; ST = Scheduled Tribe; SEBC = Socially and Educationally Backward Classes. PwD = Persons with Disability; GC = Green Card; ES = Ex-Serviceman. In 2026, the additional categories are included in base categories. In 2025, standalone PH, GC and ES entries do not state a base category; PH maps to PwD.

        Management and quota. The management pool is separate from regular seats. Its 2026 category split is not supplied; all 48 management seats in 2025 are explicitly UR. “Unspecified” means the base reservation category is not stated, including standalone PH, GC and ES entries in 2025. MD/MS retains its source quota blocks. The diploma and DNB documents do not state a quota type; they are labelled “Not stated”.

        Institution names. FM MCH, Balasore is grouped with FM MC; PRM MCH, Baripada with PRM MC; BB MCH, Balangir with BB MC. DHH Bolangir, DHH Puri and DHH Baripada remain distinct from their medical-college counterparts.

        Specialty names. O&G and Obst. & Gyn. are grouped as Obstetrics & Gynaecology. Paediatrics is displayed as Pediatrics; Anaesthesiology as Anesthesiology. TB & CD remains separate from Respiratory Medicine.

        Filtering. Institution and other filter choices follow the selected programme and the other active filters. Comparison choices include institutions listed in either 2025 or 2026. Switching programmes clears incompatible specialty, institution, quota and seat-pool selections, while retaining compatible selections. The Government / Private selection is retained. Selections within the same category group use OR. The reservation and special-category groups combine with AND, as do different filters. Reservation and additional category filters count allocations matching the same source entry. For 2025, a combined base and additional category is n.a. where the source does not state that relationship. Without those filters, totals use the printed seat counts. A dash in the college matrix means no matching allocation is listed.

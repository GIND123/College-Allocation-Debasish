At a glance

The seven things every candidate should know before opening the portal.

Who runs it

Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, using a state rank list built from NEET PG 2026 ranks. Apply only at cee.kerala.gov.in.

What it covers

State quota seats in Govt MCs and RCC, plus all seats in self-financing colleges (incl. NRI & minority). AIQ is run by MCC, but AIQ joiners in Kerala Govt MCs must follow this prospectus too.

Seat matrix

Not yet out College-wise and category-wise distribution (Annexure III & IV) “will be notified later”.

Rounds

Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3 → Stray vacancy. Each state round runs after the matching MCC round.

Course length

3 years. 2 years if you hold a 2-year PG Diploma in the same subject (passed by 30/04/2026).

The big catch

Getting a seat in Phase 3 locks you out of all further counselling (state & AIQ). Leaving after the stray round costs ₹50 lakh.

After the course

General merit (incl. AIQ) candidates owe 1 year of Senior Residency to Kerala. Service candidates owe 10 years.

Eligibility

Academic

  • MBBS (NMC recognised), registered with NMC or a State Council.
  • Internship complete on or before 30/09/2026 Corrected 13/10/2026
    The notification first said 30/04/2026; CEE’s corrigendum of 13 Oct changed it to 30/09/2026.
  • Kerala State Medical Council registration needed before admission.

Age

  • No upper limit for general candidates.
  • Medical Education Service Quota: max 49 (as on 31 Dec).
  • Health Service / Insurance Medical Service Quota: max 47.

Nativity — any one of

  • Indian citizen of Kerala origin.
  • OCI cardholder — treated like Indian citizens but only for General (UR) and NRI seats; no reservation.
  • Non-Keralite who did MBBS in Kerala — eligible, but no communal / special / PwD reservation, and must sign the 1-year bond.
  • Non-Keralite in-service doctors can apply under service quota (Kerala HC, WP(C) 24607/2023).

Layered on top

  • PwD: 5% horizontal, split inside each category. Minimum 40% benchmark disability; a State Level Medical Board decides fitness per course. UDID card + affidavit accepted. No certificate upload at application — just tick the box.
  • Ex-Serviceman: 1 seat.
  • Dependent of Jawan killed in action (else dependent of Ex-serviceman): 1 seat.

Rules that trip people up

  • SEBC (27% total) needs a Non-Creamy Layer certificate for Kerala State Education purpose from the Village Officer. A plain community certificate gets fee concession only, not reservation.
  • SC/ST certificate must be from a Tahsildar, in the candidate’s own name, issued for this purpose.
  • Inter-caste children: pick one parent’s SEBC community in the form — it can’t be changed later.
  • Fake Non-Creamy Layer certificate → cancellation + up to 2-year debarment.

How to apply

You don’t have to finish in one sitting, but all 5 steps must be done before the deadline.

Get ready before you start

Tap to tick off.

The 5 steps

  1. Register — NEET roll number, rank and date of birth; add mobile and a password (8+ characters with a letter, a number and a symbol). Note down the Application Number — you need it until allotment.
  2. Fill application → click Final Submission. No changes after this.
  3. Pay fee — the “Pay Application Fee” tab turns green when payment succeeds.
  4. Upload photo, signature and certificates — opens only after payment. Images can’t be changed after “Finish Upload Image”.
  5. Print the acknowledgement page and keep it. Do not send it to CEE.
Session timeout: stay idle too long and you’re logged out — unsaved data is lost. Keep documents ready before you begin.

Counselling rounds (CAP)

The order of your options is everything: if you’re allotted an option, you must take it or lose all your options.

Phase 1 Fresh options

Pay a security deposit of ₹10,000 (₹5,000 for SC/ST/OEC & concession groups), refunded after counselling. If allotted, join or lose everything. Options below your allotted one are deleted automatically.

Phase 2 No new options

You can only confirm, delete or re-order existing options. Confirmation is compulsory to stay in. Happy with your Phase 1 seat? Don’t confirm (or delete all) — your seat stays safe.

Phase 3 Lock-in round

Fresh options again. Registration fee = annual tuition of the seat (Govt only) or ₹1,00,000 if private seats are included (half for concession groups). Adjusted in fees if you join; forfeited if allotted and you don’t join. Seats get converted between categories here. Anyone allotted in Phase 3 is reported to MCC and barred from further state and AIQ rounds.

Stray vacancy ₹50 L penalty

Fresh options. Vacating any joined seat after this point = ₹50 lakh liquidated damages, revenue recovery, and up to 2 years’ debarment.
Tip from the prospectus itself: only list colleges you will genuinely join, save often, don’t hit refresh/back, and don’t open the page in multiple tabs.

Who studies free?

  • SC / ST candidates (merit or reserved seats).
  • OEC candidates (Annexure I) — need community or Non-Creamy Layer certificate.
  • Annexure II communities with family income ≤ ₹6 lakh (income certificate for 2026‑27).
  • Children of registered fishermen; inmates of Sree Chitra / Nirbhaya / Juvenile Homes.
Concessions don’t apply to NRI or minority quota seats, the application fee, or the option registration fee.

The bond

General merit & AIQ candidates

  • 1 year as Senior Resident (or Lecturer / Asst. Surgeon etc.) in Kerala, immediately after results.
  • Can be relieved for a DM/MCh; total bonded service then capped at 2 years.
  • Released if you get a regular government job elsewhere (with proof).
  • Absolved if the Government doesn’t offer a post within 1 year.
  • Original certificates are held by the college until the bond is done.

Service candidates

  • 10 years (or till retirement), with 2 years in difficult/remote areas right after the course.
  • Breaking it: pay total salary for the obligatory period, plus stipend at 8% interest.

Signing

  • ₹200 Kerala stamp paper, all pages. Two sureties (parents/guardian; spouse allowed for service).
  • Must be executed within 7 days of joining or admission is cancelled.

Life as a PG (Junior Resident)

  • All 365 days are working days; 1 weekly off (not cumulative).
  • 20 days casual leave/year, max 7 at a stretch.
  • Up to 15 days special leave for CMEs/workshops.
  • 80% attendance needed each year separately.
  • Absent without leave > 10 days → termination + damages.
  • No leaving India without DME sanction.
  • No private practice of any kind.
  • Maternity stipend once during the course.
  • Anti-ragging & dowry undertakings mandatory.

Document checklist

CEE notice, 07‑10‑2026: applications open soon. Get reservation/concession certificates now. Only certificates uploaded with the online application by the deadline will be considered, and each must be within its validity period.

Nativity proof — options

  • Kerala origin: SSLC / Indian passport / birth certificate (yours, or a parent’s with relationship proof) showing birth in Kerala, or a Village Officer nativity certificate. Without these: born in Kerala + 5 years’ continuous study in Kerala + self-declaration.
  • Non-Keralites with Kerala MBBS: certificate from the college Principal (format on the CEE website).

NRI quota — 4 documents

  1. Sponsor’s passport copy + visa / green card / Norka Roots ID / OCI, clearly showing the sponsor’s job. Visa must be valid up to the Phase 3 allotment.
  2. If the job isn’t shown on those papers: sponsor’s employment certificate attested by the Indian Embassy/Consulate.
  3. Relationship certificate from revenue authorities, explaining the exact link. If the sponsor is father/mother, school records showing both names are enough.
  4. Sponsor’s affidavit on ₹200 stamp paper, notarised, promising to pay all course expenses (tuition and special fees).

Relaxations: UAE-type Resident ID or Golden Visa accepted without embassy attestation; embassy-attested papers valid for 6 months.

Watch-outs & gaps in the document

Internship cut-off is 30/09/2026. The notification first printed 30/04/2026; CEE corrected it to 30/09/2026 on 13/10/2026. Candidates finishing later are not eligible.
File sizes conflict. Certificates: the prospectus said under 300 KB, the application portal allows 10–500 KB. Images: official papers say 1–100 KB in one place and 10–100 KB elsewhere. Safest: images 10–100 KB, certificates under 300 KB.
Old year references. The FAQ and How-to-Apply guide still mention “NEET‑PG 2025 / PG Medical 2025” in places — NEET‑PG 2026 is correct.
Email address mismatch. The notification and payment guide use ceekinfo.cee@kerala.gov.in; the upload guide shows a different address. Use the helpline if unsure.
Beware unofficial copies. Some copies of the notification going around carry a WhatsApp “paid counselling” line on every page. That is not from CEE — use only cee.kerala.gov.in.
Seat matrix missing. Annexure III & IV are “to be notified later”, so nobody can yet see how many seats each college/subject/category has.
Fee may change. Fees are from a 2023 order and marked “subject to change”; self-financing fees aren’t published yet.
Phase 3 is a one-way door. A Phase 3 allotment ends your AIQ chances too — decide your AIQ vs Kerala priority before then.
About the CEE notice of 07‑10‑2026 (CEE/208/2026‑TA2)

New compared with the prospectus

  • Strict cut-off: certificates not uploaded by the application deadline won’t be considered for reservation or NRI quota.
  • Each certificate must be within its validity period — last year’s certificates may fail.
  • NRI sponsor’s visa must remain valid up to the Phase 3 allotment.
  • Minority quota: a Village Officer community certificate is needed if the school certificate doesn’t show community.
  • Nativity certificate must state the place of birth.
  • If the NRI sponsor is a parent, school records with both names are enough to prove the relationship.

Not covered by the notice

  • No application dates were given (the 12‑10‑2026 notification later set the deadline as 16 Oct, 4 PM).
  • EWS, Ex-service, fishermen’s children and Home inmates aren’t mentioned — their certificates are still required under the prospectus.
  • GO 243 is printed as “2024” though dated 06.08.2014 (prospectus correctly says 243/2014).
  • Issued only in Malayalam — non-Keralite and NRI applicants may miss it.
Drafting errors noticed (worth checking with CEE)
  • Cover page leaves the approving G.O. number blank (“G.O……”).
  • Bond forms XV and XVI still refer to “PG Medical Prospectus 2025‑26 / 2025”.
  • Bond XVI cites “G.O.(P) No.016/2017/Fin” — the actual deputation order is No.106/2017/Fin.
  • Annexure index labels Annexure IV as “2024‑25”; the annexure itself says “2025‑2026”; Annexure III heading says 2025.
  • PwD assessment refers to NMC interim guidelines for AY 2025‑26.
  • Annexure numbering in the index is out of order and repeats numbers.
  • SC/ST leftover rule says “SC ↔ ST → OEC → State Merit”, but the conversion table only lists SC ↔ ST; the bullet below it confirms the OEC step.
  • IIMS Palakkad lecturers’ inclusion in MESQ is still pending in the Supreme Court.

Notes

  • All figures are provisional and may change after final NMC approval.
  • SGRR All-India Open: the PDF prints a total of 68, but its rows add up to 88. This page uses 88.
  • SGRR NRI: OBG and Paediatrics each list 2 seats but only 1 "Recognized". This page counts 2 each (16 total, as printed).
  • All-India Open here means the private colleges' open seats in state counselling. Government colleges' All-India seats are filled through MCC and are not included.
  • VCSG Srinagar in-service seats (UK PMHS doctors only, not counted above): Diploma ENT (DLO) – UR Open; Diploma TB & Chest – UR UK Women; DNB General Surgery ×2 – UR UK Women, SC UK Women; DNB Orthopaedics – UR Open.
  • Spelling slips in the PDF (e.g. "OPRN", "OEPN", "UK OPEN") are treated as Open. Repeated seat numbers in the PDF (HIMS Anatomy, GBCM Ophthalmology, Almora Paediatrics) are kept as separate seats.
  • DPW, FF and other sub-category rules are set by the Uttarakhand roster order (G.O. 124, 22 May 2020). Check the official brochure for eligibility.
Source: Uttarakhand State NEET-PG Centralised Counselling 2026 provisional seat matrices (All-India Open, State Quota, NRI Quota).

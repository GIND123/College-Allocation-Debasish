Bihar PGMAC 2026: Counselling Guide

Plain-language summary of BCECEB Advt. 01 & 02 (18.10.2026) and the official seat matrix.

What is PGMAC 2026?

Bihar's state-level counselling for MD / MS / PG Diploma / DNB seats in government and private medical colleges of Bihar. It's run by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB), and ranks are based on NEET-PG 2026 results.

386Govt MD/MS seats (state quota)
648Private MD/MS seats
20Govt PG Diploma seats
95DNB seats for in-service Bihar doctors

About 1,150 seats in total. There will be 4 rounds, all online.

The 3 things every student must know

Picking even one private college raises your security deposit to ₹2,00,000. If you only want government seats, choose the government-only option (₹25,000 or ₹12,500).
Round 1 is safe. You can leave a Round-1 seat with "Free Exit" and keep your deposit.
Round 2 is binding. Resigning or not joining a frozen seat means you lose your deposit. If you exit Round 1, you must fill fresh choices for Round 2.

    When is the deposit returned?

    After all rounds end, to the account you paid from, if you got no seat or if you joined your allotted seat. The account can be yours, your father's or your mother's.

    When is it lost?

    • Resigning a Round-2 seat
    • Not reporting to a seat you froze in Round 2
    Private college tuition fees are separate and high. Check each college's fee on the BCECEB or college website before applying. The Board does not take complaints about private fees.

    Round by round

    Round 1

    • If allotted, upload documents, then choose Freeze or Float.
    • Free Exit allowed (17–18 Nov): leave without losing your deposit.
    • After exit or resignation, old choices are cancelled. You must fill new choices for Round 2.
    • If you got no seat, your choices carry over automatically, and you can still edit them.

    Round 2

    • No new registrations (unless new colleges or seats are added).
    • You cannot remove the seat you got in Round 1 from your choice list.
    • Resigning or not joining a frozen seat: deposit forfeited.
    • Float from Round 2 to Round 3 is allowed.

    Round 3

    • If you joined a seat in Round 1 or 2 and didn't resign, you cannot take part.
    • Round-3 allottees must report in person with fees.

    Government colleges

    Women can take both general and women-reserved (F_) seats in their category. Men can take general seats only. RCG seats are for backward-class women only.

    Private colleges

    Private colleges have no caste reservation. Only UR, Minority and NRI. KMC Katihar & Madhubani MC: 50% Muslim minority. MGM Kishanganj: 30% Sikh minority. Non-Bihar residents can't get private seats using reserved-category cut-offs.

    Branch summary (MD/MS)

    Other seat pools

    • Govt PG Diplomas (20): PMC Patna (TB & Chest, DMRD, DPMR, Microbiology) and DMC Darbhanga (DA). Half open, half for doctors with remote-area service.
    • DNB post-MBBS (35): in-service Bihar government doctors only. Sadar hospitals, medical colleges, BIMHAS Koilwar, LNJP Patna, Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital.
    • DNB Diploma (60): in-service only. Mostly DCH and DGO at district Sadar hospitals, plus DA, DMRD, DLO, DO.

    Documents checklist

    Upload clear PDF scans when downloading your allotment order. Bring originals for verification.

    • Residential certificate
    • Caste certificate / EWS certificate (if applicable)
    • College leaving certificate
    • Internship completion certificate
    • MBBS passing certificate & mark sheets (Parts I, II, III)
    • Medical registration certificate
    • NOC from employer (if employed)
    • Matric certificate
    • NEET-PG admit card
    • Aadhaar card
    • Remote/difficult area service certificate (if claimed)
    • In-service Govt of Bihar certificate (if required)
    • DQ certificate + UDID card + affidavit, merged into one PDF (if DQ)
    • PGMAC 2026 rank card
    • Printed confirmation page
    • Check Slip (2 copies) + Biometric Identification Report Form (1 copy)
    • 6 photographs (same as uploaded)
    Blurry, wrong or mismatched documents can cancel your seat. Re-upload quickly if you get an SMS or email about a problem.

    Mistakes to avoid

    • Mobile number and email can't be changed. They come from your NEET-PG form, and all OTPs go there.
    • Missing 28 Oct means missing counselling. There's no fresh registration for Round 2.
    • Payment failed but money deducted? Pay again right away. The failed amount comes back later.
    • Lock your choices so you can print them. Unlocked choices auto-lock at the deadline.
    • Fill many choices. Many college-branch pairs have only one seat, which goes to a single category.
    • Don't add a private college casually. It raises your deposit to ₹2 lakh.
    • Exiting Round 1? Refill choices. Otherwise you won't be considered in Round 2.
    • Keep your password private. The Board isn't responsible for misuse.
    • Don't wait for the last day. The Board isn't responsible for internet or bank failures.

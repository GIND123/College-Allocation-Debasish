What the bond requires

  • 2 years of government service after PG — as Senior Resident, Assistant Professor, Junior Specialist or Medical Officer, wherever the state posts you.
  • Original certificates (including MBBS and PG degrees) stay with the college until the bond is cleared.
  • If you don't serve, you and your surety are jointly liable for the full amount, recoverable as arrears of land revenue.
  • Signed on stamp paper of at least ₹500, with a surety and two witnesses.

2. In-service candidates

State-quota MD/MS and DM/MCh seats for doctors already in Rajasthan government service

  • ₹80 lakh for a degree course — the same for every branch.
  • Serve the government for at least 5 years after the course, if the government requires it.
  • If you don't serve, you and your surety are liable for ₹80 lakh, plus interest at government loan rates and legal costs.
  • Stamp paper of at least ₹500, one surety (a doctor, with designation) and two witnesses.

3. Seat-leaving bond — ₹10 lakh (everyone)

Submitted at the time of reporting · applies to both non-service and in-service candidates

You pay ₹10 lakh if you:

  • don't join the allotted college by the deadline,
  • leave the course before completing it, or
  • have your admission cancelled for poor performance, misconduct or indiscipline.

At joining: a ₹1.5 lakh bank guarantee + an ₹8.5 lakh surety bond, with two sureties. If unpaid, the college can keep your original certificates.

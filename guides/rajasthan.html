📝 How to apply

Part 1 — basic details + fee

  • Roll no., name, parents' names, DOB — exactly as in NEET PG 2026. No "Dr." before the name.
  • Domicile, category (General/SC/ST/OBC-NCL/MBC-NCL), STA, EWS, PwBD, internship completion date.
  • Mobile & email are fetched from NEET PG result.
  • Pay fee → get Application ID & password. Save them.
Part 1 cannot be edited after payment.Category and EWS cannot be changed later. Check everything before clicking "Proceed to pay fee".

Part 2 — login at pg.neetrajasthan.com

  • Address, nationality, MBBS details & marks, number of attempts.
  • Service status — in-service candidates upload employer NOC.
  • Ten compulsory declarations.
  • PCPNDT choice: Yes/No for the 6-month ultrasound course.
  • Upload documents, preview each one, then Final Submit — form locks after this.

After that

  • Pay security deposit → choice filling opens.
  • Print 4 copies of the application form after the last date; paste photo, sign, write place & date.

⚠️ Issues found in the official documents

  • Private-college page is dated 2025. Its footer says "NEET PG 2025", "Board-2025" and "20.11.25". Confirm a 2026 private matrix on the official site before paying ₹5 lakh.
  • Two total errors. Anaesthesia shows 234 (should be 101 + 149 = 250). Anatomy shows 45 (should be 32 + 24 = 56). The grand total of 2,434 is correct.
  • Copies of application form: the schedule says 2, the guidelines say 4. Carry 4.
  • Deposit closes before choice lock on 29 Oct (5 PM vs 11:45 PM). Pay early.
  • 6 Nov is tight: letter print by 1 PM, fee by 3 PM, reporting by 4 PM. Pay tuition during banking hours.
  • Declaration 5 says the Board "shall be responsible" for password misuse — likely a missing "not". Never share your ID, password or OTP.
  • Seat matrix is provisional — recheck before choices lock.
Beware of "paid counselling" WhatsApp numbers.The circulated PDFs carry an advertisement for paid guidance. It is not from the Board. Download notices only from the official websites below.

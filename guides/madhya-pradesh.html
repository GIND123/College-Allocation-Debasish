    EWS

    • Chose UR in NEET PG but qualify as EWS in MP? Select EWS when registering and upload an MP EWS certificate for 2025-26.

    NRI quota (private colleges)

    • Minimum NEET PG cutoff required.
    • Caste/social reservation does not apply to NRI seats.
    • NRI candidates can also compete for open seats, with a single registration.

    What happens to the deposit

    • Join the seat: counted toward your college fees.
    • No seat allotted: refunded to your bank account.
    • Seat allotted but not joined: forfeited.

    Round-by-round rules

    Round 1

    • Didn't join your seat, or got no seat? You stay eligible for later rounds.

    Upgrade option

    • When joining, you must answer Yes or No to "upgrade in the next round". The admission slip is generated only after you answer.
    • Said Yes and got upgraded: your old seat is cancelled automatically, and you must join the new one.
    • Said Yes but no upgrade: you keep your current seat.
    • Want a different college in Round 2? Say Yes at joining, then fill and lock choices again.

    Round 2

    • Pay the deposit, then fill and lock fresh choices.
    • Allotted but not joined: out of later rounds, deposit lost.

    Mop-up round

    • Open to: those with no seat in Rounds 1–2, new registrants, and joined candidates who opted for upgrade.
    • Vacant seats after mop-up are filled as per Rule 13 of the MP admission rules.

    Resigning a seat

    SituationResult
    Leave a Round 1 seat up to 2 days before Round 2Still eligible. Refund minus 10% (max ₹10,000; NRI ₹25,000 deducted)
    Leave a Round 2 or mop-up seatNot eligible for later rounds
    Leave up to 2 days before mop-upNo seat-leaving bond
    Leave after mop-up startsSeat-leaving bond applies

    Practical steps

    • Registration opens before Round 1, and again after Round 2 (before mop-up).
    • Registration and choice filling are fully online. Visit the college only after allotment.
    • Fill at least 1 choice; there is no maximum. Always lock your choices.
    • On allotment, report in person with the allotment letter and documents (Annexure-3 / Form-1). Pay full fees online after verification.
    • Large payments can be split across several RTGS/NEFT transactions, with a separate challan for each amount.
